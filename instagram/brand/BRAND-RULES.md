# Final Touch (@finaltoucch): brand rules (owner-approved)

## ONE design system, everything in unison (owner rule)
- **Master slide template = "History's strangest mysteries" (Higgsfield job `428e0031-615d-476f-bc4a-e148467547fe`).** Pass it as a reference on EVERY slide and EVERY thumbnail, and copy its exact font, sizes and colours.
- One font family everywhere: the bold clean sans from the master headline, with the same lighter version for body text. Never mix fonts. (Anton is retired.)
- Slides: full-bleed photo with no card, white border or rounded corners. Dark gradient over the lower half. Small white "@finaltoucch" top-left and page "NN/TT" top-right. Optional huge outlined glowing lime number. Bold white headline, left-aligned, then light-grey body. Labels are plain lime text, never pills. CTA slide: white "Comment" with a lime KEYWORD, and "Save • Share • Follow" in lime.
- Thumbnails: the same font, centered, layered behind his head (approved example: Day 1, job `2020eeda-5a2c-4a1e-b4e9-8a4dfcdf7a09`). The big word goes on top, with the feature word as an echo ×3 (solid, faded, very faded); never write "%" numbers.
- Colour: **black #0A0A0A on light/white backgrounds**, **lime #C6FF00 (soft glow) on dark**, white for headlines on slide gradients.

## Thumbnails
- Full, uncropped photo. Never add bands, bars, boxes or panels, and never cut off heads, faces or hands.
- Text never covers a face.

## Face, wardrobe, voice
- Always pass the owner's real reference photos so the face matches him. Black thick-frame glasses in every shot; a different nice outfit per scene.
- Voice: Higgsfield element "Finaltouch-" `f20af7a3-2416-4cb8-b394-688e71bcc43d`, speech_rate +30, loudness +10, with a music bed and sound effects.
- Never name a city; say "right here from my home".
