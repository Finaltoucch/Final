## The story

**Title:** *She Left Him Alone at Prom Without a Word… 2 Years Later He Found 63 Letters His Mother Hid | Full Movie*

**Alt title:** *He Waited for Her at Prom in the Rain… She Was Fighting for Her Life Across Town*

**Setting:** Atlanta, Georgia. A public high school, a small family house, an Atlanta children's hospital (unnamed, invented), and a college football stadium.

**Promise:** She broke his heart on purpose, so he wouldn't give up his future to watch her die. The viewer knows the truth from minute one; he doesn't for two years.

### The cast (six roles)
| Role | Character |
|---|---|
| Humble hero | **Nia Carter** (17, later 19). A Black girl with a bright, gap-toothed smile, natural curls (later a short buzz from chemo, then short curls grown back), always sketching in a notebook. Works after school at her aunt's braiding salon. |
| The love | **Jalen Brooks** (18, later 20). A tall, handsome Black young man, close fade, a small scar through his left eyebrow, star wide receiver with a full scholarship offer. Gentle and shy off the field. |
| Rival | **Kiara**, a popular cheerleader who wants Jalen. She spreads the rumour that Nia "left for an older guy". |
| Hidden villain (blood tie) | **Denise Brooks**, Jalen's mother. A single mom who worked double shifts for his scholarship. She hides all of Nia's letters "to protect his future". Not evil: afraid. Her confession is the gut punch. |
| Wise ally | **Pops**, Jalen's grandfather, a retired bus driver. He's the one who finally tells Jalen where to look. |
| Heart at home | **Mama Carter**, Nia's mother, a nurse's aide who sleeps in the hospital chair every night. |

### The secret the viewer knows from the hook
The night before prom, Nia was diagnosed with leukemia. She knew Jalen would turn down his scholarship to stay with her. So she texted him "I can't do this anymore. Don't look for me." and never came. Then she wrote him a letter every week from the hospital: 63 letters. Denise intercepted every one.

### Why it can go viral
- **Dramatic irony in 45 seconds:** every scene where he's angry at her hurts, because the viewer knows.
- **A hidden sacrifice** (she gave him up for his future) and a **blood-tie villain** (his own mother) who is sympathetic, not cartoonish. That splits the comments ("his mom was wrong" / "I understand her"), which drives engagement.
- **A clippable object:** the 63 letters and the dried corsage. Both work as thumbnails and Shorts.
- **A mirror ending:** the same gym, door and corsage from the hook, reversed.
- **The thumbnail:** Jalen in a navy suit at the gym door with the corsage, glancing back; on the other side of a gold split, Nia in a hospital bed holding a stack of letters. No text, matching the style that worked.
