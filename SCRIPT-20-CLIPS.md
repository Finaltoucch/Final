# ACT ONE

## 1 · THE MORNING RUN — 0:00
Dawn, empty coastal road, low hard sun off the water. **RYAN** running easily, grey vest,
no kit, no weapon. Long lens, he comes toward camera and past it. Cut wide: one man on a
long empty road with the sea beside him.

*No dialogue.* He is fit, calm, and has nothing to do. It is the only time in the film he
looks like this.

---

## 2 · THE CALL — 0:30
He slows, hands on knees, breathing. Phone out of the armband. He listens.

**VOICE ON THE PHONE** *(thin, compressed, never seen)*
> There is an old man in a fishing village who says he has something you should see.

He straightens up. Looks out at the water.

**VOICE ON THE PHONE**
> Ryan. He asked for you by name.

He does not answer. He starts walking, then walks faster.

## 3 · THE VALUATION — 1:30
A dealer's back room. Black velvet. The loupe. **THE BUYER**, sixty, examines Armando's
rough uncut diamond for a long moment while Ryan waits.

**THE BUYER**
> Where did this come from.

**RYAN**
> Does it matter?

**THE BUYER** *(setting the loupe down, looking at him properly for the first time)*
> If there is one, no. If there are more, it matters a great deal.

He slides the stone back across the velvet and does not let go of it.

**THE BUYER**
> Bring me a handful of these and neither of us works again.

## 4 · THE DECISION — 2:00
A quiet back room, afternoon. Ryan and **MARCUS**, civilian clothes, no weapons. On the
table: the curling photograph of the island, and the rough uncut diamond beside a set of
car keys, so its size is plain.

**MARCUS** *(picking it up, turning it once)*
> Where is it.

**RYAN**
> Three days upriver. There is an island.

**MARCUS**
> And you want seven.

**RYAN**
> Seven ways. Three days in, three days out.

Marcus looks at the photograph a long time. Puts the stone down. Sits back.

**MARCUS**
> You will need somebody to tell you when you are pushing it.

**RYAN**
> That is what I am asking.

## 5 · THE PLAN — 2:30
A rented room over a dock, night. A river chart weighed down with a torch. Ryan traces the
route with one finger. **MAYA** and **MARCUS** watching, three more people seen only as
backs in the foreground.

**RYAN**
> Boat to here. On foot from the creek. The hole is two klicks inland.

**MAYA**
> And if we meet anybody?

**RYAN**
> There is nobody to meet. Nothing has lived there since nineteen seventy-four.

**MAYA** *(not looking up from the chart)*
> That is not the same sentence.

## 6 · MATEO SAYS GOODBYE — 3:00
A doorway, afternoon, quiet street. **MATEO**, kit bag on his shoulder, crouches down to
his daughter's height. **ELENA** in the doorway behind, arms folded.

**MATEO** *(to the child)*
> Six days. You count them on your hand and then you start again.

He kisses the top of her head, stands, and looks at Elena.

**ELENA**
> Say it properly.

**MATEO**
> Six days.

**ELENA**
> That is not what I asked you to say.

He does not answer her. He picks up the bag and walks to the gate.

## 8 · NIGHT ONE — 5:45
The team around a lamp on the boat deck. **LIAM** filming everything. **DANIEL** counting

# ACT TWO — THE ISLAND

## 9 · THE 1974 CAMP — 7:00
A clearing that was a camp. Tent frames standing, canvas long gone. Wooden crates
stencilled with a company name and **1974**. Ryan, Maya and Marcus move through it slowly.

And bones. Human. Not buried, not arranged — where they fell, gone to moss.

**MARCUS**
> Nobody came back for them.

**MAYA** *(crouching by the nearest)*
> Nobody came back at all.

## 10 · THE TORN PAGES — 7:30
Maya lifts a swollen expedition log book out of a crate and opens it. A few pages of neat
handwriting — dates, depths, weights. Then a stub of torn paper. Then another. Then
nothing but stubs, all the way to the back cover.

**MAYA**
> Somebody wrote it all down.

**RYAN**
> And?

**MAYA** *(turning the book so he can see)*
> And then somebody tore it out. Every page after the fourteenth.

## 11 · MATEO GOES TO THE WATER — 8:00
Deep jungle night, firelight behind him. Mateo walks down the muddy bank with two canteens,
talking back over his shoulder to the camp.

**MATEO**
> Two years of braces. You know what that costs?

He crouches at the edge and pushes a canteen under. Bubbles.

**MATEO**
> It costs a house. My daughter has a house in her mouth.

He watches the bubbles, half smiling, thinking about something else. Flat black water in
front of him.

---

## 12 · THEY LOSE HIM — 8:30 · **ACTION**
The water erupts. Mateo is snatched backwards into the river to the chest — fast, no
warning. **KOFI** and **MARCUS** come down the bank at a sprint.

Kofi gets both hands around his forearm. Marcus grabs his rig. For four full seconds the
three of them are locked, boots sliding in the mud, water boiling white around them, the
two on the bank hauling with everything they have.

**A length of dark wet scale rolls once through the churn between them — a body thicker
than a man, gone again before the eye can fix it.**

Mateo is not screaming. He is looking at them.

Then he is gone. Straight down, out of their hands, and both men fall backwards into the
mud with nothing. His rifle floats up on its sling and turns.

**MARCUS**
> MATEO!

## 13 · THE ARGUMENT — 10:00
The riverbank, minutes later. Torches. Maya has her pack half on her shoulder.

**MAYA**
> We go back to the boat. Now, tonight, in the dark.

**RYAN**
> We are one day out.

**MAYA**

**MAYA**

**MARCUS**

**RYAN**

## 15 · LIAM — 12:15 · **ACTION** · *SHE COMES OUT OF THE TREES*
The far bank, minutes after the crossing. **OPEN ON LIAM'S FACE**, alone, on dry ground,
crouched over the drone controller reviewing the footage, grinning. Three full seconds on
him so the audience knows exactly who he is.

He looks up to call to the others — and stops. He is looking at something above him.

**LIAM**
> Ryan.

Torch beams whip upward into the canopy. **In the branches directly over him, twenty feet
up, a length of wet dark body is already moving through the leaves — a section of her, lit
in fragments as the beams swing, gone behind foliage, showing again further along.**

She comes down out of the tree in one movement.

Liam is taken off the ground and upward. His boots leave the leaf litter. The drone
controller drops and lands screen-up in the mud, still playing the footage back.

Branches thrash. Leaves come down. Then nothing.

**MARCUS** *(rifle up at the canopy, finding nothing to aim at)*
> Where is he?

Ryan turns in a full circle under the trees with his torch. Empty branches. The controller
lying in the mud with her image still moving across the little screen.

*Blood is visible on the leaf litter and on the men but the violence is never graphic.*

## 16 · DANIEL RUNS — 13:15 · **ACTION**
Underground. **DANIEL**, alone, sprinting flat out through a narrow rock seam, head torch
swinging, water exploding around his boots, hitting the walls, breath tearing out of him.

He looks back over his shoulder while running. The beam sweeps the passage behind him —
empty wet rock.

He turns forward again — **and a length of her slides out of a black side-seam in the wall
directly ahead of him, crosses the passage at chest height, and is gone into the opposite
seam.** She does not strike at him. She simply passes across his path, close enough to
touch, and continues on her way as though he is not there.

That is worse. He goes down hard, scrambles up, and runs.

He goes down hard, scrambles up, keeps running.

*No dialogue. Only his breathing, his boots, the water and the rock.*

## 17 · DANIEL STOPS — 13:45
He stops. Back against the rock, hands on knees. His torch is on the floor where he dropped
it, throwing light sideways across the water. The passage behind him is silent and empty.

He laughs once, with relief.

Then his hand goes to his chest. He slides down the rock into the shallow water, sitting.

He fumbles a rolled sock out of his pocket and tips one rough uncut diamond into his palm.
He looks at it. He closes his fist around it.

He stops moving.

**MAYA**
> LEFT! LEFT!

They burst into a wider gallery. The passage ahead necks down to a four-foot gap.

**RYAN**
> MOVE!

## 19 · THE GATE — 16:45 · **ACTION**
A stone channel running waist-deep and fast. A rotted timber gate. A rusted hand winch.
Spray on the lens. **MAYA** has both hands on the handle, hauling it round one click at a

The timber begins to fail. Boards split.

**RYAN** *(from the channel, reaching back)*
> MAYA!

**MAYA** *(not looking at him)*
> GO.

The gate lets go all at once. The wall of water takes her off her feet and she is gone

The chamber goes quiet except for something very large moving across stone.

**RYAN** *(whispered)*
> One more klick.

He goes into the crack.

# THE ATTACK VECTORS — NO TWO THE SAME

Finaltouch: *"it must not attack from water, it can attack from the trees, from inside the
bush, inside the cave etc."*

Two deaths from the water read as one death twice. Each is now a different mechanism from a
different direction:

| | Death | Where she comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Mateo** | riverbank, night | **UP OUT OF THE WATER** — the only water attack in the film |
| **Liam** | far bank, under the trees | **DOWN OUT OF THE CANOPY** — twenty feet above him, vertical, he is lifted off the ground |
| **Daniel** | the passages | **ACROSS HIS PATH, OUT OF A WALL SEAM** — she does not attack him at all. She passes, indifferent, and his heart stops |
| **Kofi** | the four-foot gap | **UP THE CORRIDOR, HEAD ON** — the only one who sees her coming and gets to shoot |
| **Maya** | the sluice gate | **NOT HER.** The water and a rotted gate kill her |
| **Marcus** | the deep chamber | **OUT OF THE FAR WALL** — she was in the room the whole time |

Water once. Above once. Beside once. Ahead once. And twice she is not the killer at all.

# NOTES ON THE VIOLENCE

**She is on screen in four of the six deaths**, and in every case as a *fragment* — a length
of scale rolling through churn, a section moving through branches, a body crossing a passage
at chest height and gone. Never a full-body wide shot. That is not squeamishness: partial and fast is what the
model renders convincingly, and it is also how real predation footage looks.

**The two she is absent from** are Daniel, who dies of his own heart with nothing touching
him, and Marcus, where we hold on Ryan's face instead. Both are stronger for it.

# NOTES ON THE FILTER

The content filter in this production fired **three times, all on the fishing village** — an
old man, a fire, and a pebble. It has never once fired on violence. A machine-gun firefight,
a man firing at the creature, a corpse with open eyes and a man dragged underwater all
passed first time.

The Benin prompts went considerably further — *"a bright line of blood opens on the dark
skin"*, *"the blade comes away wet with blood"* — and generated without trouble. Every one
of them closed with the same sentence, which is included in each violent clip above:

> **Blood is visible on their skin and clothing but the violence is never graphic.**

Rejections are refunded, so a filter hit costs time, not credits.
