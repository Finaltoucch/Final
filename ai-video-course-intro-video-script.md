## Recording tips
- Record in a quiet, well lit spot. Face a window if you can.
- Keep energy up. Speak like you are talking to one friend, not a crowd.
- If you fumble a line, pause and say it again. You will cut it later.
- Film the B-roll (screen recordings, the Telegram group, the result screenshots) separately, then lay it over your voice.
- Keep the first 5 seconds punchy. That is what decides if people keep watching.
- Post the final video at the very top of the sales page, right under the hook.
