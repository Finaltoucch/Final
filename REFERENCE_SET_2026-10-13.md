## WHAT THE INSPECTION CAUGHT
Every plate was opened at full 1536 × 2752, not as a thumbnail. Three failed and
were re-rolled:

1. **Tom's collar.** "Worn thin and slightly frayed" rendered as genuinely
   shredded, with loose threads standing off the collar edge. That destroys the
   premise — the whole point is that he looks normal to everyone, including his
   wife. Re-prompted with the collar described as *crisp and intact*, and the
   words torn / frayed / ragged / shredded banned outright.
2. **Ellie's hair.** Specified light-brown, returned light auburn — too close to
   Maisie's ginger, and those two share the final shot, where the audience must
   read them instantly as two different people. Re-prompted as cool ashy
   mid-brown with red, auburn, ginger and blonde all banned by name.
3. **Elsie's chair.** Her plate returned a beige wing chair while the day-room
   plate returned the specified dusty blue. She sits in that chair in five of
   the ten shots. The room was the better image, so the chair moved to her.

Nothing else was changed. The six plates that passed were left alone.

**Cost:** 16 images × 2.00 = **32.00**. That includes 4.00 lost on two plates
generated before the client's casting direction arrived, and 6.00 on the three
corrections above. Balance after: **6,026.83**.
