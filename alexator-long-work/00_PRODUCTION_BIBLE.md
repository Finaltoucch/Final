## Concept

A woman in her late fifties plants trees alone on a bare upland hillside at dawn.

The film is built on one reveal. For most of its length she is small against an enormous
empty slope, doing the same four movements over and over — dibber in, sapling in, heel the
soil, step. On the drop the camera lifts away from her and keeps lifting, and the scale of
what she is up against becomes clear: the whole hillside is bare, and there is one of her.
It reads as futile.

Then the light breaks and the camera turns across the valley. The opposite slope is dense
mature woodland — the same terrain, the same ridge line, the same rock outcrops, twenty
years of the same work. Last frame: she looks at it, then bends and plants the next one.

**The meaning:** labour whose payoff you will not personally witness. No dialogue, no text,
no impossible image.

## Differentiation from the two delivered films

| | RIDE HOME | FORGE | THE LONG WORK |
|---|---|---|---|
| Subject | boy, ~10 | woman, early 30s | woman, late 50s |
| Time | warm afternoon | night | cold dawn |
| Space | suburban exterior | industrial interior | open upland landscape |
| Palette | golden pastel | orange on black | wet green / grey → gold |
| Motion | travel, cycling | percussive craft | repetition, then a lift and a turn |
| Register | mood lifting | mastery | consequence over time |

Nothing is reused: new character, new environment, new light, new palette, new structure.

## Realism doctrine

Every element is ordinary physical reality — soil, roots, rain, mist, sunrise, mature trees.
The twenty-year jump is a cut to a wide shot, exactly as a documentary would do it. Nothing
supernatural, nothing physically impossible, no invented lighting.

Materials that carry the film: wet black peat, root fibre, moss, lichen on rock, waxed
cotton, breath in cold air, mist moving through trunks, low sun through cloud.

## Consistency anchors

Two reference sheets are generated once and passed as `image_references` into every keyframe:

1. **Character sheet** — the planter. Holds one identical performer across all shots.
2. **Location sheet** — the valley, in two panels: the bare near slope and the forested
   opposite slope, sharing an identical skyline, saddle notch, three rock outcrops and
   stream line. This is what makes the final reveal read as *the same place*, and it is the
   lesson carried over from the FORGE spiral.
