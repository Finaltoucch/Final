### 3. The AI feature shown *on* his body
He makes the tool's feature visible inside the photo:
- **Remove People:** he's covered in a flat green selection mask, with a mouse cursor on him.
- **Clean Up:** cars behind him are highlighted in red selection masks, with a cursor.
- **Color Grading:** his face is split down the middle, warm on one side and cool on the other.
- **Edit Video:** three clones of him, one with an angel halo, one normal, one with devil horns and a red suit.
- **LumaLabs Skills:** paint exploding on his white T-shirt.
- **CCTV:** a fisheye security-camera view of him being chased by a dog.
- **AI Depth Map:** "Depth Map" text labels floating next to him while he points.

**Why it works:** viewers *see* what the tool does before they press play.

### 4. Hero prop held toward the lens
A big foreground object with him slightly behind it:
- a glowing water bottle (Gemini Omni)
- a sunscreen tube pushed at the lens (Seedance 2.5)
- a burger mid-bite (Lovart)
- a lantern
- a tennis ball
- juggled lemons
- a Polaroid of him

Forced perspective makes the object huge and the shot feel 3D.

### 5. Real "creator" frames (UGC / authority)
These are plain camera shots of him talking:
- holding a phone with a lav mic clipped on (Screen Swap)
- on stage with a microphone (Seedance)
- pointing at text (Depth Map)
- leaning in to camera (GPT6)

These are the "trust" posts between the wild ones. They work as UGC-style Reels.

### 6. Supporting cast and story moments
Police and a masked villain (ElevenLabs V4), a zombie chasing him (Magnific), a friend in "AI is perfect", a girl in the Polaroid. Each cover is a **frame from a story**, not a portrait.

### 7. Non-human covers (about 15%)
A product or object is the hero, with no face:
- a glowing phone (Magnific Brand Kit)
- a raspberry in a water splash (GenSpark)
- neon audio panels (Claude SFX)
- a phone dropping (Pixel 3D)
- a giant eye (Match Cut)
- a goblin (Seedance)

These break up the face rhythm so the grid doesn't feel repetitive.

### 8. Camera and light make it look real
- **Lenses:** wide 24mm or fisheye for action, 50–85mm with shallow depth of field for portraits.
- **Angles:** low angle (heroic), bird's-eye, Dutch tilt for chaos.
- **Lighting matches the scene:** golden-hour sun, neon magenta stage, warm tungsten interior, cold blue night. His skin always takes on the scene's light colour, which is the #1 realism cue.
- **Real-photo texture:** skin pores, film grain, slight motion blur, contact shadows under his feet.
- **Mid-action, never posed:** shouting, jumping, biting, saluting. A candid frozen moment.

### 9. Typography layered *into* the scene
- **The font:** a heavy, rounded geometric sans in neon yellow with a soft glow. Tool name big; feature smaller in an **echo stack** of 3 copies fading out.
- **Depth layering:** text sits **behind his head or body**. Examples: "Higgsfield" behind the guard's hat, "LumaLabs" behind him at the table, his cap overlapping "GPT6", "Clean Up" behind his head.
- **Placement:** text goes in the empty sky, wall or ground. The **face is never covered**.
- **Echo direction:** the stack runs downward or upward, matched to empty space.

### His constant
**The cream Vans cap.** It appears even with most costumes and is dropped only when a costume demands it (helmet, cowboy hat). It's his visual logo.

## Part 2 — Your version

### Your constant: the black thick-frame glasses
Wear them in **every** generated image, even with costumes (a Roman soldier in glasses is funnier and more recognisable).

**Clothes change every post.** Use stylish, well-fitted outfits that match the scene. Your reference photos all show one black shirt, so **every prompt must name a specific outfit**; otherwise Higgsfield copies the black shirt. See the wardrobe guide below.

### Prompt formula (Higgsfield, Nano Banana Pro or Cinema Studio Image 2.5, 4:5 or 9:16)

```
Photorealistic cinematic photograph of <<<16f2a137-e826-4f98-ace2-112924a73993>>>
wearing his black thick-frame glasses and [SPECIFIC OUTFIT — never leave blank], [ROLE / COSTUME],
[ACTION frozen mid-motion], [EXPRESSION],
in [SETTING], [SUPPORTING CAST / PROPS].
Shot on [LENS] from a [ANGLE], [LIGHTING] with the light colour falling on his skin,
natural skin texture and pores, subtle film grain, shallow depth of field,
contact shadows, candid moment, not posed.
Leave clean empty space in the [TOP/BOTTOM] third for a headline.
Vertical [4:5 / 9:16].
```

### Wardrobe guide (rotate, never repeat back-to-back)
| Vibe | Outfit ideas | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| Smart creator | Cream or olive overshirt over a white tee, tailored trousers, clean sneakers | Tutorials, UGC |
| Luxury | Tailored suit in navy, burgundy or camel, open collar or turtleneck | Money, clients, ads, mission posts |
| Streetwear | Oversized graphic tee, cargo pants, bomber jacket, chunky sneakers | AI video tricks, action scenes |
| Afro-modern | Contemporary agbada, kaftan or patterned Ankara shirt, modern cut | Mission and Africa posts, standout covers |
| Studio | Knit polo, chinos, minimal watch | Desk, setup and Q&A posts |
| Costume | Whatever the role needs (referee, explorer, cloak) | Role-play covers |

### Cover finishing (done in code with the `design/` templates)
1. Generated photo as the full background.
2. Cut-out of you (Higgsfield background removal) placed on top of the text layer. That gives the "text behind my head" depth.
3. Headline in lime `#C6FF00` with a heavy rounded font and soft glow, plus the feature word in an echo stack of 3.
