## Designer brief

- **Palette:** product teal (#4FB8BE approx) + warm neutral skin tones. Red reserved exclusively for the price banner and the under-eye annotation.
- **Type:** bold geometric sans, white fill + black stroke for captions over footage; red banner uses white text.
- **Caption rule:** two lines max, bottom third, never covering the eye area — the eye area is the proof.
- **Price lockup:** `$40` struck through, `$20` at 1.6× size. One lockup, used everywhere. Never ship `$20` without the `$40` anchor.
- **Cut rhythm:** hold ~2.6s average (v1's rhythm — it worked). Slow to 4s through the results section so the skin reads as continuous and unedited.

## Pre-launch checklist

- [ ] Offer parity: ad · end card · landing page all read `$40 → $20`
- [ ] Midnight deadline is genuine and enforced on the page
- [ ] Dermatologist-tested substantiation on file
- [ ] 90-day guarantee term confirmed
- [ ] "Results vary" superscript legible in the results section
- [ ] No before/after comparison frames anywhere in the cut
- [ ] No profanity in audio or burned-in captions
- [ ] Caption spellcheck — "My skin looked brighter and smoother"
