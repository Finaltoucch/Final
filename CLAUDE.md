# Project memory: Final Touch (@finaltoucch) Instagram content

Follow `instagram/brand/BRAND-RULES.md` for every piece of content. Key rules:
- **Anton font for THUMBNAILS ONLY.** Carousel slides keep their existing font; don't change it.
- Black text on light backgrounds, lime #C6FF00 on dark.
- No cropping, bands or boxes on thumbnails.
- Always use the owner's real photos for the face, and his Higgsfield voice at +30 speed with music and sound effects.
- Never name a city ("right here from my home").
