26 bars · 1079 frames @24fps · 44.958 s · 12 shots

## MARINE LIFE: NONE
Decided 2026-10-04. Dense fish schools morph, duplicate and smear in every
current video model, and that artifact class is exactly what the client
flagged on the previous film. The descent is carried instead by light shafts,
suspended marine snow, falling colour temperature and the diver's own motion.
If one large slow creature is ever wanted (a ray crossing below him in shot
07), it is a one-line prompt change to a single shot, not a rebuild.

## CAMERA DISCIPLINE
The camera only ever travels DOWN or holds. It never rises, never cuts to a
rising move, and never looks back up at the boat after shot 05. Direction of
travel is the spine of this film.

Every prompt states its camera position as its FIRST line.

## SPEC BLOCKS

## THE SHOTS

**01 — HOOK (0–82)** · refs: diver
CAMERA: locked extreme close-up, lens about 25 cm from the mask, slightly
below his eyeline. The sea and the bright sky are curved across the glass.
ACTION: his gloved hands pull the mask strap down over the back of his head
and settle it; his eyes come into focus behind the glass.
NOTE: this is the 1–2 second hook. It must read instantly as *diving*.

**02 — THE BOAT (83–165)** · refs: boat, tender
CAMERA: wide, from the water, low and level with the gunwale, about 8 m off
the port side. Open sea to the horizon, no land anywhere.
ACTION: the diver sits on the gunwale fully geared, back to the water. The
TENDER stands behind him on the deck, one hand flat on the top of the
cylinder, checking it. Neither man speaks.

**03 — THE BREATH (166–248)** · refs: diver
CAMERA: medium, from just off the boat's side, slightly below him, looking up
past his shoulder.
ACTION: he bites the regulator, puts one palm flat over the mask and the
regulator to hold them, and looks DOWN at the water between his fins. He is
completely still. The TENDER's hand enters frame and taps him once on the
shoulder.
MUSIC: this shot sits in the kick drop-out. Stillness here is the point.

**04 — THE ENTRY (249–331)** · refs: diver · **LANDS ON THE DROP**
CAMERA: fixed, half-submerged at the waterline, the split between air and
water across the middle of the frame.
ACTION: he rolls BACKWARD off the gunwale and hits the water. A hard white
burst of bubbles fills the frame and the surface closes over.

**05 — UNDER (332–414)** · refs: diver
CAMERA: underwater, 2 m below him, looking UP.
ACTION: the bubble cloud thins and clears. He rights himself, finds his
balance, and the bright surface and the dark underside of the hull sit above
him. Last time the boat is ever seen.

**06 — DOWN (415–497)** · refs: diver
CAMERA: from below and behind, looking DOWN past his fins into deepening blue.
ACTION: he turns head-down and starts finning. Light from above falls behind
him. Marine snow drifts upward through the frame.

**07 — THE SHAFTS (498–580)** · refs: diver
CAMERA: side-on, travelling down WITH him at exactly his speed so he stays
still in the frame and only the water moves. (Camera-lock rule.)
ACTION: he fins steadily downward through broad shafts of surface light that
rake across him. The blue is noticeably darker than shot 06.

**08 — THE TORCH (581–663)** · refs: diver
CAMERA: three-quarter behind, travelling down with him.
ACTION: the water has gone blue-black and the surface light is gone. He
unclips the torch from his hip and switches it on. The beam becomes the only
light in the frame.

**09 — THE SWEEP (664–746)** · refs: diver
CAMERA: behind his shoulder, looking along the beam.
ACTION: the beam moves slowly across a bare pale seabed — sand, a low rock
shelf, nothing else. Marine snow drifts through the beam. Emptiness is the
point of this shot.

**10 — THE EDGE (747–829)** · refs: chest, diver
CAMERA: low, close to the sand, looking along the seabed.
ACTION: the beam crosses a shape and comes back to it. One corner of the
chest stands proud of the sand — dark wood, one rust-orange iron band, a
crust of barnacles. The rest is still buried.

**11 — CLEARING IT (830–954)** · refs: chest, diver
CAMERA: over his shoulder, close on his gloved hands.
ACTION: he fans the sand away in slow clouds, the domed lid and the iron
bands emerge, and he gets both hands under the lip of the lid.

**12 — THE LID (955–1078)** · refs: chest, diver
CAMERA: low and front-on to the chest, the diver behind it.
ACTION: the lid lifts. GOLD LIGHT floods up out of the chest into the black
water and lands on his mask and his chest. Hold on his face lit from below.
NO marine life in this shot — nothing competes with the gold.
