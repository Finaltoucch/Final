## Keyframes

**01 · `0.000` · HOOK**
> Extreme macro, vertical, near-total darkness. A bed of dead grey coals and fine ash filling
> the frame in a blacksmith's forge at night. One single ember has woken — a small crawling
> line of orange heat spreading slowly across the grey ash, throwing a tiny pool of warm light.
> Everything beyond it falls to black. Shallow depth of field, 100mm macro lens, the individual
> texture of ash and cracked coal razor sharp. The only light in frame is the ember itself.

**02 · `3.529`**
> Vertical interior, dark blacksmith's workshop at night. Still-life detail — a heavy hammer
> resting on a scarred anvil, tongs hanging on hooks, fine dust drifting through a single thin
> shaft of cold blue-grey moonlight from a high window. Deep shadow everywhere else. In the
> mid-ground, softly out of focus, the woman from the reference image is tying her heavy brown
> leather apron. Cold palette, one warm ember glow low in the corner of frame. 35mm lens.

**03 · `7.058`**
> Vertical, dark workshop. The woman from the reference image works a bellows handle, body
> weight behind it. The forge bed answers — coals blooming from dull red to bright orange,
> heat haze rising, sparks lifting. Warm orange light climbs up her apron and across her face
> from below while cold blue-grey moonlight edges her shoulder. Same face, same wardrobe.
> Real fire behaviour. 35mm lens, shallow depth of field.

**04 · `10.587`**
> Vertical close-up. A plain square steel bar held in tongs sinking down into the glowing bed
> of coals, the metal already beginning to take colour from dull grey to deep red at its tip.
> Coals part around it, sparks lift. The woman's gloved hand and forearm at the edge of frame.
> Blinding orange heat at the centre, black at the edges. 50mm lens, shallow depth of field.

**05 · `12.352` · THE VOID**
> Vertical. The woman from the reference image draws the steel out of the fire and holds it
> up, completely still. The bar is **white-hot** at its working end, so bright it blows out to
> pure white and throws hard light across her face, her safety glasses and the leather apron.
> Everything else in the workshop falls to pure black around her — she and the glowing steel
> are the only things visible. Absolute stillness, held breath, total concentration. Same face,
> same wardrobe. 50mm lens.

**06 · `14.117` · THE DROP**
> Vertical, the instant of the first hammer strike. The hammer head meets the white-hot steel
> on the anvil and a violent burst of orange sparks explodes outward across the whole frame,
> hundreds of individual glowing particles trailing light. Her gloved hands grip hammer and
> tongs firmly. Her face beyond, lit hard orange from the impact. Frozen at peak impact,
> high-shutter-speed look, every spark distinct. 35mm lens.

**07 · `17.646`**
> Vertical, mid-swing. The woman from the reference image hammers the glowing steel on the
> anvil, hammer raised, body committed, a fresh spray of sparks still falling from the previous
> strike. The steel glows orange-yellow where it has been struck. Warm light fills the lower
> frame, deep black above. Both hands correct and firm on the tools. Same face, same wardrobe.
> Slight motion blur in the hammer. 35mm lens.

**08 · `21.175`**
> Vertical tight close-up of the woman's face from the reference image, lit hard orange from
> below by the glowing steel just out of frame. Absolute concentration, jaw set, eyes narrowed
> behind clear safety glasses, sweat and soot on her skin, loose strands of hair stuck to her
> temple. Cold blue-grey rim light on the far side of her face. Every pore and texture visible.
> 85mm lens, background completely black and soft.

**09 · `24.704`**
> Vertical macro on the anvil face. The steel bar, now glowing deep orange, has been drawn and
> curled into a clean abstract spiral form under the hammer. Scale and fine hammer-marks
> visible on its surface, heat shimmer rising off it. The hammer head resting at the edge of
> frame. Blinding warm glow, black surround. 100mm macro lens.

**10 · `26.469` · THE BREATH**
> Vertical. The woman from the reference image has stopped working. She holds the glowing
> orange spiral up in the tongs at eye level and looks at it, head slightly tilted, the faint
> beginning of satisfaction on her face. Everything quiet and still. The warm glow lights her
> face and the underside of her safety glasses; cold moonlight behind her. Same face, same
> wardrobe. 50mm lens, shallow depth of field.

**11 · `28.233` · QUENCH**
> Vertical. The glowing orange spiral plunges into a dark water trough and a violent white
> cloud of steam erupts upward and outward, filling the upper frame, backlit orange from the
> forge behind. Water boiling and churning around the metal. Her gloved hands on the tongs.
> Dramatic, physical, real steam behaviour. 35mm lens.

**12 · `35.292` · RESOLVE**
> Vertical. The finished iron spiral, now cooled to dark blue-black with faint heat colours
> still in the metal, resting in the woman's gloved hands as she turns it slowly to look at it.
> Behind her the forge has died back to a low warm glow and the workshop settles into darkness
> and drifting steam. Quiet satisfaction on her face. Warm low key light, deep shadow, the last
> thin shaft of cold moonlight. Same face, same wardrobe. 50mm lens, shallow depth of field.
