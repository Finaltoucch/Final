## ⭐ Pitch A (recommended): "The Girl From the River"

**Title:** *He Fired the Waitress at His Engagement Party… He Didn't Know She Was the Girl Who Saved His Life | Full Movie*

**Promise:** She saved his life when they were kids and asked for nothing. Twelve years
later another woman is wearing her story, and he's marrying the liar while the real one
serves the wine.

### The six roles
| Role | Character |
|---|---|
| Humble hero | **Amara** (25), hotel banquet waitress who studies nursing at night. A long scar on her left forearm. Cares for her grandmother. Quiet faith. |
| Kind powerful lead | **Julian Hart** (30), shipping-company heir and CEO. Polite to staff, haunted by the night he nearly drowned. Still searching for "the girl from the river". |
| Glamorous cruel rival | **Sienna Vale**, his fiancée, always in red. She found the old newspaper story and claimed to be the girl. Cruel to staff in public ("People like you pour, they don't talk"). |
| Hidden villain | **Marcus Hart**, Julian's charming cousin and CFO. He paid Sienna to play the girl so that, once married, her votes give him control of the company. |
| Wise ally | **Mr. Bello** (60s), the old head waiter. Strict with Amara at first, then her protector. He was the hotel porter who carried the two soaked kids out of the river that night. |
| Heart at home | **Nana Efua** (70s), Amara's grandmother, with a heart condition and an **$860/month** prescription. "Love that has to lie is not love, my child." |

### The secret the viewer knows from minute 2
Twelve years ago, during a flood, 13-year-old Amara jumped into the river and held a
drowning boy against a fallen tree until help came, cutting her arm open on the metal.
To keep him awake she kept saying: **"Hold on. I'm not letting go."** She tied her red
bead bracelet to his wrist so he wouldn't drift. He woke in hospital with only the
bracelet. She never came forward because her grandmother was sick and the newspapers
scared her.

Only the real girl knows the words. Julian has never told anyone except his fiancée, and
**Sienna gets them wrong**.

### Why it should travel
The viewer knows the truth from minute 2, so every time Sienna is thanked for saving him,
it hurts. Impostor-takes-the-credit is one of the most commented romance hooks on YouTube
("I KNEW it was her"), the pool scene is a clean, clippable climax, and the mirror ending
reverses the exact humiliation from the hook. Small cast, two main locations (hotel and
Nana's flat) plus one river night.

**Production notes:** the river prologue is short (4–5 shots, Seedance, night, rain,
no gore on the cut, just a dark sleeve). The pool fall uses the proven collapse recipe:
wide shot, slight slow motion, realistic weight. Child actors in the prologue must not
resemble the adult leads beyond hair and skin tone.

## Pitch B: "Married for the Inheritance"

**Title:** *He Paid the Poor Nurse to Marry Him for One Year… She Never Cashed the Check*

- **First 60 s:** A lawyer's office. A billionaire heir signs a one-year marriage contract
  with a hospital nurse so he can inherit. He slides her a check for $500,000. She folds it
  into her uniform pocket. Cut to that night: she puts it, uncashed, in a Bible.
- **The secret:** She never takes a cent. She married him because his late father, her
  patient, asked her with his last breath to "look after my stubborn boy".
- **The gut punch:** His stepmother freezes his accounts and he loses everything. The day
  the contract ends, he finds her packing his things into her small flat, not hers into a
  taxi. The uncashed check falls out of the Bible.
- **Mirror ending:** In the same lawyer's office, he tears up the contract and asks her to
  marry him for real, with his father's recorded last wish played to the family.

## Pitch C: "The Cleaner His Daughter Chose"

**Title:** *The Widowed CEO's Little Girl Called the Cleaner "Mommy"… His Fiancée Was Furious*

- **First 60 s:** At the CEO's engagement party, his 6-year-old daughter runs past the
  fiancée and into the arms of the office cleaner, shouting "Mommy!". The room goes silent.
  The fiancée slaps the cleaner. Title card, then "THREE MONTHS EARLIER".
- **The secret:** The cleaner lost her own baby the same week his wife died, in the same
  hospital. She's been secretly sewing the girl's school costumes and leaving them as
  "from Mommy in heaven".
- **The gut punch:** The daughter goes missing at a fair; the fiancée did nothing; the
  cleaner finds her asleep with the handmade costume clutched in her arms.
- **Mirror ending:** At the wedding meant for the fiancée, the daughter walks the cleaner
  down the aisle herself.
