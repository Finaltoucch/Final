## Design decisions worth keeping

**Canopy inflation is never rendered.** The pull happens in the cut between 09
and 10 — free fall to already-under-canopy across one frame. Canopy inflation is
the least reliable physics available to these models; the edit deletes the risk
and the roar-to-silence cut is stronger than the shot would have been.

**Altitude corrected from the brief.** The concept specified 30,000 ft. That is
a HALO jump requiring oxygen and a sealed face mask, which would have hidden the
face the film depends on in shots 03 and 09 — and an unmasked jump from that
altitude is not survivable, which breaks the standing realism rule. Built at
~13,000 ft, which is where sport skydiving actually happens and what a
five-jumper turbine aircraft is for.

**An aircraft reference sheet was added mid-project** after the client noticed
shot 07 would invent a plane matching nothing else. The sheet exists purely so
the aircraft he falls away from is the aircraft he was sitting in.
