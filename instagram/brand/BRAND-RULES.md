# Final Touch (@finaltoucch): brand rules (owner-approved)

## ONE design system, everything in unison (owner rule)
- **Master slide template = "History's strangest mysteries" (Higgsfield job `428e0031-615d-476f-bc4a-e148467547fe`).** Pass it as a reference on EVERY slide and EVERY thumbnail, and copy its exact font, sizes and colours.
- One font family everywhere: the bold clean sans from the master headline, with the same lighter version for body text. Never mix fonts. (Thumbnail titles use the condensed master font from the thumbnail master; slides use the master slide font.)
- **ALWAYS blend the image into the background:** the photo fades smoothly into the dark text area through a soft gradient, like the History slide. Never use a hard edge, a split line, a separate panel or a rounded box behind the text.
- Slides: full-bleed photo with no card, white border or rounded corners. Dark gradient over the lower half. Small white "@finaltoucch" top-left and page "NN/TT" top-right. Optional huge outlined glowing lime number. Bold white headline, left-aligned, then light-grey body. Labels are plain lime text, never pills. CTA slide: white "Comment" with a lime KEYWORD, and "Save • Share • Follow" in lime.
- **THUMBNAIL MASTER = `brand/MASTER-thumbnail-higgsfield-camera.webp` (Higgsfield job `bd818442-3f59-42cf-a8ef-69b73ace6efa`, imported media `c371f486-9fd7-4711-b84d-40f1537c50d1`).** Every thumbnail copies it exactly: heavy condensed all-caps (Anton-style); the text block fills the full width with equal side margins, and each line is stretched to the same width; **NO repeated words / no echo stack**: just the title lines (usually 2), each once, CENTERED as a tight BLOCK (every line stretched to the same width, justified); text sits in the top half with his head layered IN FRONT of the lower lines; never write "%" numbers.
- Colour: **black #0A0A0A on light/white backgrounds**, **lime #C6FF00 (soft glow) on dark**, white for headlines on slide gradients.

## Thumbnails
- Full, uncropped photo. Never add bands, bars, boxes or panels, and never cut off heads, faces or hands.
- Text never covers a face.

## Face, wardrobe, voice
- Always pass the owner's real reference photos so the face matches him. Black thick-frame glasses in every shot; a different nice outfit per scene.
- Voice: Higgsfield element "Finaltouch-" `f20af7a3-2416-4cb8-b394-688e71bcc43d`, speech_rate +30, loudness +10, with a music bed and sound effects.
- Never name a city; say "right here from my home".

## Never touch the photo
- Adding a title must not change the background, lighting, colours or framing. Keep comfortable side margins (about 8% each side); never run text edge to edge.
- Set titles in code on the original image (Anton font file + a cutout of him for the head-in-front layer). Do NOT ask AI image models to draw titles: they repaint the photo and drift the font.

## THUMBNAIL MASTER (latest, overrides earlier thumbnail rules): `brand/MASTER-thumbnail.webp` ("AI ADS / $0 BUDGET", job 45faca0c-280f-44b0-94a2-6701e14b52a7)
- Font: heavy, wide, bold geometric sans (Inter Black / Helvetica Black style), all caps. Not condensed.
- Line 1 = big title. Then line 2 written 3 times stacked: 100%, ~70%, ~40% opacity (slight fade).
- Every line is stretched to the SAME width, forming a justified block centered horizontally, with side margins of about 7.5% each.
- The top margin is the SAME on every thumbnail (the top of the text sits about 6% from the top). Tight line spacing.
- His head sits in front of the lower faded lines. The photo is otherwise untouched.
- Colour: lime #C6FF00 with glow on dark backgrounds, black #0A0A0A on light.
