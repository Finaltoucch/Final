# THE ACTION PROBLEM — AND THE REBALANCED SPINE

Finaltouch: *"how can there be only 2 action scenes in a 12 minutes video?"*

## The measurement

The current 11:52 assembly:

| | Seconds | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Dialogue scenes | ~500 | **70%** |
| Walking, arrival, atmosphere | ~150 | 21% |
| **Action** — Kofi's corridor, Marcus firing | **60** | **8%** |

Eight percent. A creature film wants 30–40%.

## The cause

The cold-open instruction *"never show her"* was widened into a film-wide law. With the
creature barred from the screen, every beat where she could have **done** something became
a beat where people **talk about** what she did. Six of the seven deaths happen off-screen
or in the gaps between scenes.

The script did not do this. The cut did.

## THE PRINCIPLE THAT WAS MISSING

**A creature film earns its quiet scenes by paying for them with violence first.** The
current cut has the quiet without the payment. Every dialogue scene in it is 15–30 seconds
too long, and every death is 30 seconds too short.

She is only barred from the screen in the cold open. Everywhere else, what she does is the
film.
