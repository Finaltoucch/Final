# EPISODE 01 — "They Killed Her Cub… She Followed Their Fires for Nine Days"

**Runtime target:** ~17 min 30 sec
**POV:** The animal. Humans are the intruders.
**Bond:** Grief → grudging debt. **No romance. No talking animals. No narrator.**
**Grade:** One LUT — bone-white / ice-blue shadows, single warm firelight source. Crushed blacks, blown highlights on snow.
**Aspect:** 16:9, 2.39:1 letterbox for the cinematic read.

**Alt titles for A/B:**
- They Killed Her Cub… She Followed Their Fires for Nine Days
- They Took Her Cub. She Never Stopped Walking.
- Nine Nights. Nine Fires. She Was Behind Every One.

---

## CHARACTER BIBLE

Paste these anchor strings **verbatim** into every prompt for that character. Do not paraphrase them — drift starts the moment you rewrite the string.

**HER (cave lioness):**
> `massive prehistoric cave lioness, pale tawny-grey coat, faint ghost rosettes, heavy scarred shoulders, amber eyes, frost crusting her muzzle, one torn ear`

**THE BOY (Vek):**
> `gaunt boy about fourteen, soot-streaked face, dark matted hair, layered reindeer-hide furs, pale spotted cub-pelt around his shoulders`

**THE LEADER (Harn):**
> `broad man in his forties, braided beard hung with bone beads, deep scar across the nose, heavy bearskin cloak, bone-tipped spear`

**THE CUB:**
> `cave lion cub, pale downy fur, faint spots, blue-grey eyes, oversized paws`

## ENVIRONMENT ANCHORS

- **THE WHITE STEPPE** — endless wind-scoured snow plain, black rock outcrops, skeletal larch trees
- **THE FALLEN GIANT** — half-buried frozen mammoth carcass, ribs arching from the snow like a cathedral *(recurring landmark — re-establish location from 3+ angles across the film)*
- **THE ICE RIVER** — frozen river, blue-black pressure ridges, cracks under snow
- **THE FIRE** — one low fire, hide windbreak, drying rack. Gets smaller every night. **This is the film's clock.**

## DIALOGUE RULE

**Five spoken lines in the entire film.** All human, all in an unsubtitled archaic tongue with **burned-in English subtitles.**

This is a deliberate production cheat: a constructed language means viewers aren't reading lips at all — they're reading text. Lip-sync scrutiny drops to near zero, and it reads as *elevated*, not cheap.

## DAY ONE — THE WALK (1:40 – 3:40)

Establish two things: she is enormous, and she is hurt.

- **Wide:** her against the scale of the steppe — a speck under a bruised sky.
- **CU:** blood freezing on her flank where a spear grazed her. She doesn't slow.
- **Medium:** she passes **THE FALLEN GIANT** — the mammoth ribs. Establish the landmark clean and wide; you'll return here twice.
- **Wide:** the hunters ahead, four dots. They stop. She stops. They move. She moves.
- **Night. Wide:** their fire, small and warm. Beyond the light, two amber points floating in the black.

**Sound:** almost none. Wind, paw-fall, distant fire crackle. **Let it be uncomfortably quiet.** The silence is what makes the roars land later.

## DAY TWO — THEY REALISE (3:40 – 5:20)

- **Medium:** a hunter drops back to check the trail. He crouches at a paw print — his spread hand doesn't cover half of it.
- **CU:** his face changing.

**SUBTITLE — HUNTER:** *"Something is behind us."*

- **Wide, handheld:** the camp packing in a hurry, fire kicked out.
- **Night. Wide:** the sentry at the edge of the firelight. He turns. Empty snow.
- **Wide (locked off, held 12 sec):** the same empty snow. Nothing happens. Hold. Hold.
- **SNAP — Medium, violent, 1.5 sec:** she takes him out of frame. One flash of pale fur.
- **Wide:** the others reach the spot. Only a spear standing upright in the snow.

**⚠️ This is your first big retention beat.** The long empty hold before the snap is doing all the work — do not cut it short.

## DAY THREE & FOUR — THE TRAP (5:20 – 8:00)

They stop being prey and start being hunters. This is where she loses.

- **Medium:** Harn drives sharpened stakes into a snowdrift, covers them over.
- **Wide:** the bait — a haunch of meat on the ice, absurdly exposed.
- **CU:** her eyes. She knows. She comes anyway.
- **Action sequence (~70 sec, no dialogue):** she circles, tests, takes it — and the ice gives. Stakes. A spear through the shoulder. She tears free and drags herself into the dark.
- **CU:** the spear shaft snapped off, still in her, working deeper as she walks.
- **Wide:** the hunters cheering at their fire. **The fire is smaller tonight.** Only three of them now.

**Sound design carries this whole sequence:** ice cracking in stages, wet impact, her breathing going ragged, the *hollow* knock of bone on bone. No score until she's clear — then a single low cello.

## DAY FIVE — THE WHITE OUT (8:00 – 10:00)

The blizzard. Both sides nearly die. **The film's quietest, most beautiful stretch.**

- **Wide:** whiteout. Visibility three metres. Two shapes moving in parallel, neither able to see the other.
- **CU:** she lies down. Snow begins burying her. Her eye closes.
- **Cut to the humans:** huddled under a hide, the fire guttering.

**SUBTITLE — HUNTER:** *"It doesn't sleep."*

- **CU:** Vek, the boy, wrapped in the pale spotted pelt. First clear look at it. **Don't linger — let the audience half-notice.**
- **Wide, static, held 20 sec:** the snow mound where she lay. Nothing. Nothing.
- Then: it moves. She stands up out of the snow like the ground itself getting up.

---

## DAY SIX — THE SHRINKING FIRE (10:00 – 11:20)

The reversal. She realises she isn't chasing hunters anymore.

- **Wide:** from her POV on a ridge — the fire below is noticeably smaller. Two figures.
- **CU:** one of them is limping badly.
- **Medium:** she watches them eat almost nothing.
- **CU:** her face. Something shifts. Hold it. Don't explain it.

> **This is your emotional turn.** Nine days of momentum, and the target is already dying. Play it entirely on her face and the size of the fire.

---

## DAY SEVEN — THE DEBT (11:20 – 13:00)

- **Wide:** open ice. Harn alone, having sent the boy ahead. He plants his spear and waits.
- **Medium:** she comes out of the white. Slow. Unhurried.
- **CU:** the bone beads in his beard, clicking.

**SUBTITLE — HARN:** *"We were starving. That is all it ever was."*

- **Medium:** he lowers the spear. Doesn't drop it — lowers it.
- **Wide (silhouette against white sky), 8 sec:** it ends. Show nothing. **Cut on the sound, not the image.**
- **Wide:** the spear standing alone in the snow. Bone beads scattered.

**Advertiser-safety note:** every kill in this film happens in silhouette, off-frame, or on a cutaway. You lose nothing — implication is stronger — and you stay fully monetisable.

## DAY EIGHT — ALONE (13:00 – 14:10)

- **Wide:** one set of footprints now. Small ones.
- **Medium:** Vek stumbling, falling, getting up.
- **Wide:** behind him, at a constant distance, she walks. Never closing. Never leaving.
- **CU:** he looks back. She doesn't hide anymore. She lets him see her.
- **Night. Wide:** he can't light the fire. His hands won't work. **No fire tonight.**

# SOUND DESIGN BIBLE

This is where the realism is won. Budget as much time here as on the visuals.

| Category | Specifics |
|---|---|
| **Snow** | Three distinct footstep types: fresh powder (soft), crust (crunch-break), ice (hard scrape). Never reuse one everywhere. |
| **Her** | Breath is the character. Layer: chest rumble + nasal + a wet edge when injured. Roar = lion + tiger + slowed-down bear, pitched down 20%. |
| **Fire** | Must audibly shrink across the film. Day 1 = full crackle and pop. Day 8 = nothing. |
| **Bone** | Beads clicking, the mammoth ribs groaning in wind, the spear shaft creaking in the wound. |
| **Silence** | Use true digital silence at three moments: shot 10, the growl stopping at 15:20, and the final black. |

**Score:** solo cello and low strings. **One melodic motif, four notes**, introduced at shot 12 (the distant fire) and only fully resolved in the epilogue. Ethereal female vocal enters exactly twice — Day Five whiteout, and the pelt reveal.
