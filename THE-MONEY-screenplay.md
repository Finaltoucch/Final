# THE MONEY

**A short film · 5 minutes · 25 scenes**

CHARACTERS
**BEN** — early 30s. Steady. Keeps things to himself and calls it protecting people.
**GABBY** — early 30s. Sharp, proud, decides fast. Being right matters to her.
**MRS. OKAFOR** — 60s. Gabby's mother. Frail, lucid, stubborn.
**R. ADEYEMI** — 50s. Care manager. Two lines.

---

## ACT ONE

---

**1. INT. FLAT — HALLWAY — NIGHT**

A suitcase, upright, packed. GABBY grips the handle. BEN stands between her and the door.

> **BEN**
> Just let me say one thing.

> **GABBY**
> You had four months to say one thing.

He doesn't move. She waits. He says nothing.

> **GABBY (CONT'D)**
> That's what I thought.

She goes past him. FREEZE.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> She was certain. She was also wrong. It took eleven months to find that out.

---

**2. EXT. ROOFTOP — GOLDEN HOUR — FOUR YEARS EARLIER**

Ben and Gabby on a blanket, wine, city behind them. She's laughing at something he said.

> **GABBY**
> That is not a real story.

> **BEN**
> Every word.

> **GABBY**
> Nobody's life is that stupid.

> **BEN**
> Mine is. You'll see.

---

**3. INT. SMALL KITCHEN — NIGHT**

Gabby cooks. Ben sits on the counter, stealing from the pan.

> **GABBY**
> Get off my counter.

> **BEN**
> It's our counter.

> **GABBY**
> It's my counter. You're a guest with a key.

He grins. She hands him a spoon without looking.

---

**4. INT. BEDROOM — MORNING**

Gabby asleep. Ben awake beside her, watching. He touches her hair — careful, so she doesn't wake.

No dialogue.

---

**5. INT. RESTAURANT — NIGHT**

Candlelight. Ben's phone BUZZES on the table. He glances at the screen. Kills it. Turns it face down.

> **GABBY**
> Work?

> **BEN**
> Nothing.

---

**6. INT. RESTAURANT — CONTINUOUS**

Gabby looks at the face-down phone. Then at him. Something crosses her face and she puts it away.

> **GABBY**
> Okay.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> She decided it was nothing. That's a decision, not a fact.

---

**7. INT. SUPERMARKET — CHECKOUT — DAY**

The card machine BEEPS. DECLINED. Ben laughs, produces another card.

> **BEN**
> Bank's being dramatic.

> **GABBY**
> Third time this month.

> **BEN**
> Work's slow.

She looks at him a second too long.

---

**8. INT. BEDROOM — NIGHT**

Dark. Both on their backs.

> **GABBY**
> Are we okay? With money.

> **BEN**
> I've got it handled.

> **GABBY**
> That's not what I asked.

He rolls over.

---

## ACT TWO

---

**9. INT. LIVING ROOM — DAY**

Ben's phone on the sofa arm, alone. It LIGHTS.

ON SCREEN: `Transfer sent — £850.00`

---

**10. INT. LIVING ROOM — CONTINUOUS**

Gabby stands over it. Doesn't touch it. Reads it. The screen dims. She stays there.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> She hadn't looked. Not yet. That mattered to her, later.

---

**11. INT. KITCHEN — EVENING**

Across the table.

> **GABBY**
> Eight hundred and fifty pounds. Who to?

Ben stops eating.

> **GABBY (CONT'D)**
> I'm not accusing you. I'm asking.

> **BEN**
> I know.

> **GABBY**
> So answer.

He looks away. Doesn't lie. Doesn't answer.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> He could have ended it here with one sentence. He'd promised not to.

---

**12. INT. FLAT — DAY**

Empty. Gabby alone in front of Ben's desk. A long moment. She opens the drawer.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> This is the part she never defends.

---

**13. INT. FLAT — CONTINUOUS**

Bank statements. She turns pages. Same amount. Same date. Again. Again.

ON PAGE: `R. ADEYEMI`

Her hands slow down.

---

**14. INT. FLAT — CONTINUOUS**

Under the statements — a photograph. A WOMAN in her fifties at a garden table, a small CHILD on her lap.

Gabby sits down on the floor, still holding it.

---

**15. INT. KITCHEN — NIGHT**

She holds up the photograph. Ben goes white.

> **GABBY**
> Who is she.

> **BEN**
> Gabby—

> **GABBY**
> Who is the child.

> **BEN**
> I can't tell you.

> **GABBY**
> You *can't*.

> **BEN**
> I can't.

> **GABBY**
> Then we're finished.

He says nothing. She waits for him to fight. He doesn't.

---

**16. INT. CAFÉ — DAY**

Gabby and a FRIEND. The photograph on the table between them. Gabby is crying and furious at once.

> **GABBY**
> Two years. Twenty thousand pounds. And a picture of them in his drawer.

> **FRIEND**
> Gabby.

> **GABBY**
> Don't. Don't do the voice.

---

**17. INT. FLAT — HALLWAY — NIGHT**

The suitcase. The doorway. As scene 1. She goes past him. He lets her.

The door closes. Ben stands in the hall.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> He didn't stop her. She's never forgiven him for that either.

---

## ACT THREE

---

**18. INT. NEW FLAT — NIGHT — ELEVEN MONTHS LATER**

Smaller. Half-unpacked. Gabby on the floor with a takeaway box, phone to her ear.

> **GABBY**
> I'm fine. Honestly. I'm fine.

---

**19. INT. NEW FLAT — HALLWAY — DAY**

Post on the mat. She opens one standing up, coat still on. Care-home letterhead.

ON PAGE: `ACCOUNT OVERDUE`

She reads it twice.

---

**20. INT. CARE HOME — CORRIDOR — DAY**

Gabby walking fast, coat flapping, past doors.

---

**21. INT. CARE HOME — ROOM 14 — CONTINUOUS**

MRS. OKAFOR in a chair by the window. Delighted.

> **MRS. OKAFOR**
> You didn't say you were coming.

> **GABBY**
> Mum. Who's been paying for this?

---

**22. INT. CARE HOME — NURSES' STATION — CONTINUOUS**

A name plate: `R. ADEYEMI — CARE MANAGER`

On the desk beside it — the same photograph from the drawer. The woman. The child.

ADEYEMI looks up.

> **ADEYEMI**
> Can I help you?

Gabby can't speak.

---

**23. INT. CARE HOME — ROOM 14 — LATER**

Mrs. Okafor takes her daughter's hand.

> **MRS. OKAFOR**
> He made me promise.

> **GABBY**
> Mum—

> **MRS. OKAFOR**
> He said you'd never take it if you knew. He was right, wasn't he.

Gabby's face goes to nothing.

---

**24. EXT. CARE HOME — DAY**

Gabby against the wall, sliding down it. Phone in her hand. BEN on the screen.

Her thumb hovers. She doesn't press it.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> She's had eleven months to think of what to say. She still hasn't called.

---

**25. INT. RESTAURANT — NIGHT**

The same table as scene 5. Ben alone. Two glasses, one untouched.

His phone lies face down. He turns it over.

Nothing.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> He kept his promise. That's what it cost him.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

## Casting the voices

| Role | Lines | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| Narrator | 8 | Callum or Cillian — detached, dry |
| Ben | 14 | Male, warm, quiet |
| Gabby | 21 | Female, sharp |
| Mrs. Okafor | 4 | Older female |
| Adeyemi | 1 | Any |

**~2 credits total** for all voice generation.

**Note on lip sync:** Cinema Studio won't match mouths to separately generated
dialogue. Two ways to handle it — keep dialogue on shots where the speaker is
off-camera or in profile, or run the whole thing narration-only and cut the
spoken lines. The narration-only version is safer and loses less than you'd think;
the dialogue above can survive as subtitles.
