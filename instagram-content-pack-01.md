# Instagram Content Pack #1

Faceless explainer page, same voice as the Bright Side–style YouTube scripts:
upbeat, curious, second-person ("you"), soft claims, no diagnosis.

**This pack:** 3 Reels · 2 Carousels · 1 Story sequence · captions, hashtags, and a 7-day schedule.

---

## Instagram Format Rules (apply to everything below)

- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 (1080×1920) for Reels and Stories; 4:5 (1080×1350) for carousels.
- **Hook in the first 1–2 seconds.** Text on screen *before* the voiceover starts — most people watch muted.
- **Burned-in captions** on every Reel (big, centered, 2–4 words at a time).
- **Safe zone:** keep text out of the bottom ~250px and right ~120px (UI buttons cover it).
- **Reel length:** 20–40s does best for this format. Loop the ending back into the start where possible.
- **Carousel:** slide 1 = hook, last slide = save/share prompt. 7–10 slides.
- **CTA priority:** Saves and shares matter more than likes — ask for those.

## CAROUSEL 1 — "7 Psychology Tricks That Make People Like You" (9 slides)

**Design:** Bold sans-serif, one idea per slide, bright background color per slide, small cartoon illustration in the corner, page number bottom-left.

| Slide | Headline | Body text |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **7 psychology tricks that make people like you instantly** | Swipe → (#4 is a game changer) |
| 2 | **1. Say their name** | People light up when they hear their own name. Use it once or twice — not every sentence. |
| 3 | **2. Mirror them (a little)** | Match their energy and pace subtly. It makes conversations feel easy. |
| 4 | **3. Ask for a small favor** | Oddly, people tend to like you *more* after helping you. It's called the Ben Franklin effect. |
| 5 | **4. Let them talk about themselves** | Talking about ourselves feels good. Ask one follow-up question and listen. |
| 6 | **5. Notice their eye color** | Not literally — it just keeps your eye contact natural and warm. |
| 7 | **6. Remember one small detail** | "How did your sister's wedding go?" beats any compliment. |
| 8 | **7. Leave on a high note** | People remember how a conversation *ends*. End with a laugh or a kind word. |
| 9 | **Save this for your next first meeting 📌** | Send it to the friend who needs it most 👀 |

**Caption:**
> Social skills are just skills — you can practice them 🧠
> Which one are you trying first? 👇
> 📌 Save this before your next interview, date, or first day.

## 7-Day Posting Schedule

Post at your audience's peak time (check *Insights → Most active times*). If you're unsure, start with 6–8 PM local time.
