## Packaging rules (apply to all 10)

1. **A number lives in the title or the thumbnail. Always.** 113, 3,000, $30, $700, $200.
   That's the asset no competitor can copy.
2. **Sentence case, never all-caps.** All-caps reads as spam to a cold viewer.
3. **Thumbnail text: 3–4 words max, readable at 120px wide.** Test it by shrinking it on
   your phone until it's thumbnail-sized. If you can't read it, rewrite it.
4. **Your face is in 7 of the 10.** You are the credential. Faceless doesn't work for an
   authority niche.
5. **Colour system — pick it once and never change it:** Upwork green (#14A800) as the
   accent, near-black background, white text, one red element reserved *only* for
   negative/warning thumbnails (#1, #6). Consistency is how people spot you in the feed.
6. **No question-mark titles.** "Are Boosted Proposals Worth It?" underperforms "Boosted
   Proposals Are Eating Your Connects." Make the claim.

# 9. The 12 questions I get every day

**Source:** your weekly Spaces — *"Come with all your questions about Upwork."*

### The angle

This is the **search workhorse** of the list. Every other video is one keyword; this one is
twelve. Chaptered properly, it can surface in a dozen different searches and keep earning
for years.

Two rules make it work. **Answer fast** — 60–90 seconds each, no throat-clearing. And
**answer at least three of them against the consensus**, because twelve agreeable answers is
a video nobody finishes. Your Spaces recordings will tell you which questions genuinely
recur; use the real twelve, not invented ones.

Likely candidates from your niche: connects worth it, what to charge starting out, does JSS
really matter, do you need a video intro, is Upwork saturated, does location hurt you,

# 10. Earning dollars from Nigeria

**Source:** the spine of your whole identity — *"hired 113 times… working from Nigeria."*

### The angle

The most underserved video on the list. Every Upwork channel is American or European, so
nobody credible covers **the actual logistics of earning from Nigeria** — and it's the thing
your core audience most needs and can least find.

Two halves. First, the **emotional question**: does being in Nigeria hurt you? Answer it
honestly. If some clients filter by location, say so — then show that 113 hires happened
anyway, and explain what actually neutralises it. Honesty here is your credibility.

Second, the **boring logistics nobody else will film**: identity verification, payment
methods that work, withdrawal fees compared, timing, the naira conversion question, tax.
Boring, high-intent, and almost zero competition. This is the video people will send to
their friends.

Warn them properly about the scam ecosystem targeting Nigerian freelancers — account
renting, fake agencies, "I'll use your profile" arrangements that get accounts permanently
banned. Nobody with your standing has said that clearly on YouTube.

**Do not give tax advice.** State what you do, then say plainly that they should check with
someone qualified.

# Publishing schedule

| Week | Video | Why here |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **#3 — 113 jobs, no proposals** | Plants the flag. Every new subscriber should land here. |
| 2 | **#1 — The $300 client** | Your widest-reach story, released once there's a flagship to catch the traffic. |
| 3 | **#5 — The $200 plan** | Converts story viewers into system viewers. |
| 4 | **#2 — $30 to Top Rated Plus** | The trust video, landing when new visitors are checking you out. |
| 5 | **#9 — 12 questions** | Search net goes up early so it compounds all year. |
| 6 | **#4 — The $700 bet** | Story beat, keeps the emotional cadence. |
| 7 | **#10 — From Nigeria** | Core-audience gift. Expect your best comment section. |
| 8 | **#6 — Boosting** | Strong opinion. Slot it where engagement is already warm. |
| 9 | **#7 — Student result** | Proof it transfers, positioned before any offer. |
| 10 | **#8 — Grading Upwork's advice** | Lightest lift; a breather week you'll be grateful for. |

The pattern underneath: **story → system → story → system.** Never two teaching videos back
to back. The algorithm and the audience both want variety, and you'll burn out filming ten
tutorials in a row.

One upload per week, same day, same time. Don't batch-publish — you'll starve week six.

## Shorts

Three per long-form, published between uploads. That's 30 Shorts from 10 videos. Every Short
ends on the same line: *"full breakdown on the channel."* Shorts are not the product; they're
the trailer.

## What to measure

After all ten, sort by **impressions CTR** and **average view duration** together:

- Low CTR, high AVD → the content is right, packaging is wrong. Retitle and re-thumbnail.
- High CTR, low AVD → the hook oversold. Tighten the first 30 seconds.
- Low on both → wrong topic. Drop it, don't rescue it.

Ten videos is roughly the point where that data means something. Before then, don't
over-read it.
