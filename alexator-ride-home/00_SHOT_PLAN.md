# RIDE HOME — ALEXATOR, *Rise Again*

Second concept. Grounded, photoreal, one earned surreal beat at the end.

**Why the change:** the first concept (GRAVITY WELL) was built on impossible physics —
rain falling upward, an ocean over a city. That is close to the worst case for current
AI video: invented physics renders as mush and reads as fake. This concept is a real
boy in a real place, doing an ordinary thing, with a single lift at the climax.

## Story

A boy walks home from school, flat and tired, white wired earphones in. The track
builds. On the drop his mood turns. He gets his bike, rides, and the joy takes over —
until the last phrase, where he lifts off the road entirely.

## Craft rules for realism

- **Late-afternoon natural light** throughout. Night is where AI video falls apart.
- **Documentary register** — 28/35/50/85mm, shallow depth of field, real skin texture,
  film grain, unretouched. Not "cinematic render".
- **His face in only three shots** (03, 05, 09 — plus 08 and 11). The rest carried by
  shoes, hands, wheels, back-of-head and the drone. Reads as better filmmaking *and*
  removes most of the ways AI faces break.
- **One surreal beat**, at the very end, earned by the whole build.
