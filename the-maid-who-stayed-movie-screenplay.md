## PART 1 — THE NEW MAID (0:00–6:00)

**1. MANSION GATE — DAY** (0:00)
*Grace, in a yellow sundress, clutches her handbag in front of a huge white mansion. She looks up at the sky.*

**GRACE** *(whispering)*: Lord, please. I need this job. Sam needs this job.

*A SECURITY GUARD walks up.*

**GUARD:** You Grace Miller?
**GRACE:** Yes, sir.
**GUARD:** Follow me.

**2. DRIVEWAY — DAY** (0:15)
*They walk past a Rolls-Royce, two Bentleys and a Ferrari. Grace's eyes go wide.*

**GRACE** *(under her breath)*: How many cars does one man need?
**GUARD** *(smiling)*: Is it that obvious you're new?
**GRACE:** Is it that obvious?
**GUARD:** You're staring at the Ferrari like it owes you money.

*Grace laughs nervously.*

**3. GRAND LIVING ROOM — DAY** (0:35)
*A massive room: chandelier, double staircase, grand piano. Grace stands in the middle, tiny.*

**GRACE** *(V.O.)*: This living room is bigger than the whole building I grew up in.

**GUARD:** Wait here.

**4. LIVING ROOM — CONTINUOUS** (0:45)
*MRS. HAYES walks in. Pearls, black dress, not smiling.*

**MRS. HAYES:** You must be Grace.
**GRACE:** Yes, ma'am.
**MRS. HAYES:** I'm Mrs. Hayes. I run this house. Have you cleaned a house like this before?
**GRACE:** No, ma'am. But I cleaned hospital rooms for two years while I was in school. Nothing is
dirtier than a hospital.
**MRS. HAYES:** *(a flicker of approval)* We'll see.

**5. HALLWAYS — DAY** (1:05)
*Walking tour. Fresh flowers, marble, paintings.*

**MRS. HAYES:** Laundry, upstairs bedrooms, kitchen when the cook is off. Three rules. Never enter
Mr. Caldwell's study. Never touch anything on his desk. And never, ever argue with Miss Pierce.
**GRACE:** Who's Miss Pierce?
**MRS. HAYES:** You'll know her when you hear her.

**6. GRAND STAIRCASE — DAY** (1:25)
*ETHAN comes down the stairs in a navy suit, reading his phone. He looks up.*

**ETHAN:** Good morning, Mrs. Hayes.
**MRS. HAYES:** Good morning, Mr. Caldwell. This is Grace. New staff.
**ETHAN** *(offering his hand)*: Ethan. Welcome, Grace.

*Grace hesitates, then shakes it.*

**GRACE:** Thank you, sir.
**ETHAN:** If anybody gives you a hard time, you tell Mrs. Hayes. And if Mrs. Hayes gives you a hard
time… *(whispers)* you're on your own.
**MRS. HAYES:** I heard that.

*Ethan grins and leaves. Grace watches him go.*

**7. MONTAGE — GRACE WORKS** (1:50)
*In her navy-and-white uniform: making beds, polishing the piano, folding towels. Quick cuts, upbeat music.*

**8. KITCHEN — EARLY MORNING** (2:05)
*Ethan pours coffee, sips, burns his tongue.*

**ETHAN:** Ah! Every single morning.
*Grace drops one ice cube into his mug.*
**GRACE:** Try it now.
*He sips. Surprised.*
**ETHAN:** That's… perfect. How did you know?
**GRACE:** You've burned your tongue three mornings in a row, sir. A man who runs three hundred trucks
should be able to wait one minute for coffee.
**ETHAN** *(laughing)*: Nobody in this house talks to me like that.
**GRACE:** I'm sorry, sir, I—
**ETHAN:** No. Don't be sorry. It's refreshing.

**9. DRIVEWAY — DAY** (2:30)
*A white Range Rover pulls up. VANESSA steps out in a red dress and sunglasses, holding eight
shopping bags.*

**VANESSA:** You! New girl! Get these upstairs. Carefully. Some of those cost more than you make in
a year.
**GRACE:** Yes, ma'am.

**10. MASTER BEDROOM — DAY** (2:45)
*Grace sets the bags down. Vanessa flops on the bed with her phone.*

**VANESSA:** Change these sheets.
**GRACE:** I changed them this morning, ma'am.
**VANESSA** *(not looking up)*: Did I ask for your opinion?
**GRACE:** No, ma'am.
**VANESSA:** Then change them.

*Grace strips the bed. Vanessa watches her like a cat watches a mouse.*

**VANESSA:** People like you should be grateful to even be inside a house like this.

*Close on Grace's hands pulling the sheet tight. Her jaw clenches.*

**11. DINING ROOM — DAY** (3:10)
*Grace wipes a long glass table. Vanessa drags one finger across it.*

**VANESSA:** This is dirty.
**GRACE:** I cleaned it five minutes ago, ma'am.
**VANESSA:** Then what's this?
**GRACE:** That's… your fingerprint, ma'am.
*Silence. Vanessa leans in close.*
**VANESSA:** Clean it again. Harder. And don't you EVER forget your position in this house.

**12. DINING ROOM — CONTINUOUS** (3:30)
*Ethan walks in, loosening his tie.*

**ETHAN:** What's going on?
**VANESSA** *(instantly sweet)*: Baby! You're home early. Nothing, she just didn't clean the table properly.
*Ethan looks at the spotless table, then at Grace, who stares at the floor.*
**ETHAN:** It looks clean to me.
**VANESSA:** You men never notice anything. Come on, I want to show you the wedding flowers.

*She pulls him away. Ethan glances back at Grace.*

**13. KITCHEN — LATER** (3:55)
*Ethan finds Grace alone.*

**ETHAN:** Grace. What really happened in there?
**GRACE:** It's nothing, sir.
**ETHAN:** You don't have to let anybody disrespect you because you work here. Not even her.
**GRACE:** With respect, sir, I need this job more than I need my pride.

*He has no answer for that.*

**14. GRACE'S APARTMENT — NIGHT** (4:15)
*Tiny kitchen. Grace on the phone with MAMA RUTH. SAM coughs in the background. A physical
therapy textbook is open on the table.*

**MAMA RUTH** *(phone)*: The inhaler went up again, baby. Three hundred and forty dollars.
**GRACE:** I'll send it Friday, Mama. Every penny.
**MAMA RUTH:** And your exam?
**GRACE:** Six months. Then I'm a licensed physical therapist and nobody tells me to clean a clean table
ever again.

**15. MANSION KITCHEN — 1 A.M.** (4:40)
*Grace studying at the kitchen island on her break. Ethan wanders in, can't sleep.*

**ETHAN:** "Neuromuscular rehabilitation." Light reading?
**GRACE** *(closing the book fast)*: I'm sorry, sir, I'm on my break, I—
**ETHAN:** Grace. Relax. You're studying physical therapy?
**GRACE:** At night. Online.
**ETHAN:** Why?
**GRACE:** Because my daddy had a stroke when I was twelve. Nobody came to help him walk again. So I did.
*(beat)* He walked me to the bus every day for six years after that.
*Ethan looks at her like he's seeing her for the first time.*
**ETHAN:** That's the best thing anybody's told me in a year.

**16. POOL HOUSE — NIGHT** (5:15)
*Grace carries fresh towels toward the pool house. Through the glass door she sees VANESSA
kissing BRADLEY.*

*Grace freezes. A towel drops.*

*Vanessa's eyes snap open and look straight at her.*

**17. LAUNDRY ROOM — MOMENTS LATER** (5:35)
*Vanessa corners Grace against the dryer.*

**VANESSA** *(quiet, deadly)*: You didn't see anything.
**GRACE:** Ma'am, I—
**VANESSA:** Your little brother. The inhaler. Three hundred and forty dollars, isn't it? I hear
everything in this house. One word to Ethan and you'll never work in Nashville again. Understood?
*Grace nods, shaking.*
**VANESSA:** Good girl.

## PART 2 — THE COLLAPSE (6:00–11:00)

**18. MANSION GARDEN — NIGHT** (6:00)
*Engagement party. String lights, a jazz band, two hundred guests. Ethan in a tux, Vanessa in a
red gown. Grace carries a tray of champagne.*

**BRADLEY** *(raising a glass)*: To my big brother! The man who has everything.
*Everyone cheers. Bradley and Vanessa exchange a look.*

**19. GARDEN — LATER** (6:25)
*Ethan takes his evening pills from a small bottle Bradley hands him.*

**BRADLEY:** Don't forget your blood pressure meds, old man.
**ETHAN:** Thanks, Brad.
*Grace notices. Something about Bradley's smile feels wrong.*

**20. GARDEN — MINUTES LATER** (6:45)
*Ethan is mid-toast when his glass trembles. His words slur.*

**ETHAN:** I want to thank… thank… everyb—

*His right hand drops the glass. It shatters. He collapses.*

**21. GARDEN — CONTINUOUS** (7:00)
*Screams. Grace drops her tray and runs, kneeling beside him.*

**GRACE:** Mr. Caldwell! Ethan! Look at me. Smile for me. *(his face droops on one side)* He's
having a stroke! Someone call 911!
**VANESSA** *(grabbing Grace's arm)*: Don't you dare! There's press here! We'll take him in our car,
quietly—
**GRACE** *(pulling free, dialing)*: He doesn't have time for quietly!
*(into phone)* I need an ambulance at 4100 Belle Meade Boulevard, stroke, thirty-four-year-old male,
symptoms started at 9:42.

*She holds Ethan's hand.*

**GRACE:** Stay with me. You hear me? You stay with me.

**22. HOSPITAL CORRIDOR — NIGHT** (7:40)
*Ethan rushed on a gurney. DR. PATEL shouting orders.*

**DR. PATEL:** Ischemic stroke, get him to CT now! Who noted the time of onset?
**NURSE:** The maid, doctor.
**DR. PATEL:** Then the maid just saved his life.

**23. WAITING ROOM — 3 A.M.** (8:05)
*Grace alone in her stained uniform. Mrs. Hayes arrives with a coat and puts it around her
shoulders.*

**MRS. HAYES:** Where's Miss Pierce?
**GRACE:** She said she'd come in the morning. She was tired.
*Mrs. Hayes says nothing, but her face says everything.*

**24. HOSPITAL ROOM — DAY** (8:30)
*Ethan wakes. Bandages, monitors. Dr. Patel and a nurse at his side.*

**ETHAN** *(slurred)*: Where… am I?
**DR. PATEL:** Vanderbilt. You had a stroke, Mr. Caldwell.
**ETHAN:** I'm thirty-four.
**DR. PATEL:** It happens. Rarely. We're running tests to understand why.

*Ethan tries to move. His right arm won't respond, and neither will his legs. Panic.*

**ETHAN:** My legs. Why can't I… WHY CAN'T I MOVE MY LEGS?

**25. HOSPITAL ROOM — CONTINUOUS** (9:00)
*Vanessa enters in sunglasses with flowers. She stops dead at the sight of him.*

**VANESSA:** Ethan…
**ETHAN:** Doctor. Tell me the truth. Will I walk again?
**DR. PATEL:** I can't promise you that. With intensive therapy, there's a chance. But it will be
months. Maybe longer.
*Vanessa sets the flowers down very slowly.*

**26. HOSPITAL HALLWAY — CONTINUOUS** (9:30)
*Vanessa on the phone, whispering.*

**VANESSA:** Months, Brad. Maybe never. I didn't sign up to push a wheelchair.
*(beat)* No. Not on the phone. Tonight.

*Grace, coming around the corner with Ethan's bag, hears every word.*

**27. HOSPITAL ROOM — NIGHT** (9:55)
*Ethan alone, staring at the city lights.*

**ETHAN** *(V.O.)*: Yesterday I ran three hundred trucks across six states. Today I can't hold a glass
of water.

*Grace knocks softly with a tray.*

**GRACE:** Mrs. Hayes sent soup. Real food. The hospital stuff could kill a healthy man.
**ETHAN:** I'm not hungry.
**GRACE:** I didn't ask if you were hungry.
*She sits on the edge of the bed and holds out the spoon. He glares. She doesn't move. He eats.*

**28. HOSPITAL ROOM — DAYS LATER** (10:30)
*Ethan scrolls his phone. Dozens of old messages; no new ones.*

**ETHAN:** You know what's funny? When I had everything, I couldn't get a minute alone. Now nobody
picks up.
**GRACE:** I picked up.
**ETHAN:** You work for me.
**GRACE:** Not today, sir. Today's my day off.

*Ethan looks at her. Really looks.*

## PART 3 — EVERYONE LEAVES (11:00–17:00)

**29. MANSION — DAY** (11:00)
*Ethan comes home in a wheelchair. There is no ramp. Two orderlies have to carry him up the
front steps.*

**ETHAN:** It looks exactly the same.
**GRACE:** That's because it is the same.
**ETHAN:** Then why doesn't it feel like mine anymore?

**30. MASTER BEDROOM DOOR — DAY** (11:20)
*Vanessa blocks the doorway.*

**VANESSA:** I had the downstairs guest room set up for you, babe. It's easier. With the stairs and
everything.
**ETHAN:** That's the old storage room.
**VANESSA:** It's *cozy*.

**31. GUEST ROOM — DAY** (11:40)
*A cramped room by the laundry. A rented hospital bed. Christmas boxes still in the corner.
Grace looks at it, then at Ethan's face.*

**GRACE:** I'll make it nice, sir. Give me one hour.

**32. CALDWELL FREIGHT BOARDROOM — DAY** (12:00)
*Bradley at the head of the table, in Ethan's chair.*

**BOARD MEMBER:** With Ethan incapacitated, the board needs stability. Bradley, as acting CEO, you'll
present Atlas Logistics' offer next month. Ninety million.
**BRADLEY:** My brother would want what's best for the company.
*He smiles. It doesn't reach his eyes.*

**33. MANSION KITCHEN — DAY** (12:30)
*Vanessa with Mrs. Hayes and Grace.*

**VANESSA:** The nurse is done. Two hundred and forty dollars a day to play with his fingers? No.
And Grace, your pay is cut in half.
**GRACE:** Half? Who's going to do his therapy?
**VANESSA:** Nobody. He's going to a facility once the paperwork's done.
**MRS. HAYES:** Miss Pierce, Mr. Caldwell has never once—
**VANESSA:** Mr. Caldwell can't sign his own name, Mrs. Hayes. I'm running this house now.

**34. GUEST ROOM — DAY** (13:00)
*Grace sets up a DIY therapy station: resistance bands, a grip ball, her textbook.*

**ETHAN:** What is all this?
**GRACE:** Your physical therapy.
**ETHAN:** You're a maid.
**GRACE:** I'm a maid who's six months from a license. Lift your right hand.
**ETHAN:** I can't.
**GRACE:** Try.
**ETHAN:** I said I CAN'T!
*He sweeps the grip ball off the table. It bounces into the corner.*

**35. GUEST ROOM — CONTINUOUS** (13:25)
*Grace picks up the ball and puts it back in front of him.*

**ETHAN:** Why are you still standing there?
**GRACE:** Because you're angry.
**ETHAN:** Then leave me alone.
**GRACE:** No.
*(she kneels so they're eye level)*
You're allowed to be angry. You're allowed to cry. You're allowed to hate every single person in this
house, including me. But you are NOT allowed to use it as an excuse to quit. You fight. You hear me?
You FIGHT.

*Long silence. His right fingers twitch toward the ball.*

**36. MONTAGE — THERAPY** (14:05)
*Days pass. Grace moves his fingers. Grace reads him the Titans scores. Grace holds up
cereal-box flashcards. Ethan grimaces, sweats, and fails, then tries again.*

**37. GRACE'S APARTMENT — NIGHT** (14:35)
*Mama Ruth at the kitchen table, peeling potatoes, glasses on her nose.*

**MAMA RUTH:** You're there before sunrise and home after midnight. For half pay.
**GRACE:** He needs help, Mama.
**MAMA RUTH:** Grace Ann Miller. Be careful with your heart.
**GRACE:** I'm only helping him.
**MAMA RUTH:** And what if he wants more than help?
**GRACE:** He doesn't. He can't. He's engaged, and he's my boss, and I'm… me.
**MAMA RUTH** *(taking off her glasses)*: Baby, I didn't ask about him.

*Grace has no answer.*

**38. GUEST ROOM — NIGHT** (15:10)
*Ethan asleep. Grace checks his pill bottle and frowns. She tips a pill into her palm and
compares it with a photo on her phone.*

**GRACE** *(whispering)*: These aren't blood pressure pills.

*She reads the tiny imprint on the pill and searches it on her phone. Her face goes pale.*

**GRACE:** These would *raise* it.

*She looks at the bottle's label: "Prepared for E. Caldwell. Filled by: B. Caldwell."*

**39. GUEST ROOM — CONTINUOUS** (15:40)
*She looks at sleeping Ethan. Brushes hair off his forehead.*

**GRACE** *(barely a whisper)*: Somebody in this house tried to kill you. And I think I'm falling
in love with you. I don't know which one scares me more.

*She leaves. Ethan's eyes open in the dark.*

**40. STUDY — NIGHT** (16:05)
*Through the cracked door, Grace hears Bradley and Vanessa.*

**BRADLEY:** Rick sent a guy to my condo, Vanessa. Four hundred grand by the end of the month or
he breaks my legs.
**VANESSA:** Then stop whining. Dr. Marsh signs the incompetency papers next week. Atlas pays ninety
million. Ethan goes to Ridgeview in Memphis. It's state-funded. Nobody visits anybody there.
**BRADLEY:** And the maid? She's always in there with him. She's too smart.
**VANESSA:** Then we make sure nobody believes a word she says.

*A floorboard creaks under Grace's foot. Silence inside the study.*

*Vanessa yanks the door open. The hallway is empty.*

## PART 4 — FALLING (17:00–22:00)

**41. GUEST ROOM — NIGHT** (17:00)
*Grace slips in, breathing hard. Ethan is awake.*

**ETHAN:** You heard them.
**GRACE:** You… you can understand everything?
**ETHAN:** I can understand everything, Grace. My mouth is slow. My brain isn't. I've been lying in
this storage closet for six weeks listening to my brother and my fiancée plan my funeral.
**GRACE:** The pills. Ethan, the pills—
**ETHAN:** I know. I saw your face when you read the label.
*(beat)* Call Harold Jennings. My lawyer. His number's in my desk. Don't trust anyone else.

**42. STUDY — NIGHT** (17:35)
*Grace sneaks into the forbidden study. She finds the number in the desk drawer, next to an old
pair of women's gardening gloves and a photo of a smiling woman.*

**MRS. HAYES** *(from the doorway)*: That was his mother. Margaret.
*Grace spins around, terrified.*
**GRACE:** Mrs. Hayes, I can explain—
**MRS. HAYES:** I heard them too, child. Thirty years I've worked in this house. *(beat)* Go make
your call. I'll stand watch.

**43. MANSION — DAY** (18:05)
*HAROLD arrives while Vanessa is at the spa and Bradley is at the casino. He sits by Ethan's bed
with a voice recorder.*

**HAROLD:** A judge won't take the word of a stroke patient and a maid against a fiancée, a brother,
and a doctor. We need them on tape.
*He hides the recorder behind the Christmas boxes.*
**HAROLD:** They think he can't understand them, so this is the one room in the house where
they'll say everything.

**44. GUEST ROOM — DAY** (18:35)
*Grace helps Ethan stand at a homemade parallel bar: two broom handles bolted between dressers.*

**GRACE:** One step. That's all I'm asking.
**ETHAN:** You said that yesterday.
**GRACE:** And you didn't do it yesterday. So I'm asking again.
*He grits his teeth and moves his right foot one inch.*
**GRACE** *(gasping)*: Ethan!
**ETHAN:** Don't make a big deal out of it.
**GRACE** *(crying, laughing)*: That was the biggest inch I've ever seen!

**45. POOL — NIGHT** (19:10)
*Ethan in his wheelchair by the pool. Grace brings a blanket.*

**GRACE:** You'll catch a cold out here.
**ETHAN:** Maybe I need to feel something.
**GRACE:** Pain isn't the only proof you're alive, Ethan.
**ETHAN:** When did you stop calling me "sir"?
**GRACE:** When you started being a pain in my neck.
*He laughs, really laughs, for the first time since the stroke.*

**46. POOL — CONTINUOUS** (19:40)
**ETHAN:** Can I ask you something? Everybody left. My fiancée, my brother, my friends. Why are you
still here? And don't say for the money, because Vanessa cut your pay in half.
**GRACE:** Somebody has to make you eat your vegetables.
**ETHAN:** Grace.
**GRACE** *(quietly)*: Because when my daddy couldn't walk, everybody left him too. I promised myself
I'd never be one of the people who leave.

*He reaches for her hand with his weak right hand. It shakes. She holds it.*

*Their faces get closer. Closer.*

**VANESSA** *(off-screen)*: Well. Isn't this sweet.

**47. POOL — CONTINUOUS** (20:10)
*Vanessa on the patio, arms crossed.*

**VANESSA:** Go home, Grace. Now.
*Grace leaves. Vanessa crouches next to Ethan.*
**VANESSA:** The maid, Ethan? Really? You're even more pathetic than I thought.
**ETHAN** *(slow, careful)*: Have you… thought about… the wedding?
**VANESSA:** Is that a joke?
**ETHAN:** Would you… still marry me?
*She stands, adjusts her bracelet, and doesn't answer.*
**VANESSA:** Good night, Ethan.

**48. GUEST ROOM — NIGHT** (20:45)
*Ethan alone, staring at the ceiling.*

**ETHAN** *(V.O.)*: She looked at me like I was already gone. And the only person who still sees me
is the one I never noticed.

**49. GRACE'S APARTMENT — NIGHT** (21:10)
*Grace lies awake. Sam is asleep beside her with his inhaler.*

**SAM** *(sleepy)*: Gracie? Are you sad?
**GRACE:** No, baby. Go to sleep.
**SAM:** Is it the rich man?
**GRACE:** …Go to sleep, Sam.

## PART 5 — FRAMED (22:00–27:00)

**50. MASTER BEDROOM — MORNING** (22:00)
*Vanessa takes off her diamond bracelet and slips it into Grace's handbag, which is hanging
by the laundry room. She smiles.*

**51. FOYER — MORNING** (22:20)
**VANESSA** *(screaming)*: My bracelet! My Cartier! Forty-two thousand dollars! Someone in this house
STOLE it!

**52. FOYER — 20 MINUTES LATER** (22:35)
*A police officer, OFFICER REYES. Vanessa fake-crying. Neighbors gathering at the gate.*

**VANESSA:** I hate to say it, Officer. Her brother's sick. Medicine is expensive. I think she got desperate.
**REYES:** Ma'am, may I check your bag?
**GRACE:** Go ahead. I have nothing to hide.

*Slow motion. Her bag is dumped on the marble. Keys, a rosary, Sam's spare inhaler… and the
diamond bracelet.*

**53. FOYER — CONTINUOUS** (23:05)
**GRACE:** No… no, I didn't… Officer, she put that there! I would never—
**VANESSA:** I won't press charges. I just want her OUT. *(loud, for the neighbors)* Everybody knows
she's been throwing herself at my fiancé. It's disgusting.

*Ethan wheels into the foyer as fast as his weak arms allow.*

**ETHAN:** She… didn't… do it!
**VANESSA** *(to Reyes, sadly)*: He's confused, Officer. The stroke. He doesn't know what's real
anymore.
**ETHAN:** I know… EXACTLY… what's real!
**REYES:** Sir, please calm down.

*Bradley appears on the stairs, smirking.*

**BRADLEY:** Six months, and this is how you repay us. Wow.

**54. DRIVEWAY — DAY, RAIN** (23:50)
*Grace walks down the long driveway in the pouring rain, carrying one cardboard box. The neighbors
whisper. Mrs. Hayes watches from the door, helpless.*

*Grace turns around once.*

*In the small guest room window, Ethan's hand is pressed flat against the glass. He dragged
himself out of the wheelchair to reach it.*

*Grace presses her hand to her heart. Then she's gone.*

**55. GRACE'S APARTMENT — DAY** (24:30)
*Grace on the phone.*

**AGENCY WOMAN** *(phone)*: I'm sorry, Miss Miller. Mrs. Pierce has called every agency in Nashville.
Nobody will hire you.
*Click.*

**56. GRACE'S APARTMENT — NIGHT** (24:50)
*Grace on the bathroom floor, crying. Mama Ruth sits beside her and pulls her head onto her
shoulder.*

**GRACE:** They called me a thief, Mama. In front of the whole street.
**MAMA RUTH:** Baby, you once drove back forty minutes to a Kroger because the cashier gave you five
dollars too much. Nobody who knows you believes that.
**GRACE:** He's alone in that room with them. With those pills.
**MAMA RUTH:** Then we pray. And then we do something.

**57. GUEST ROOM — NIGHT** (25:30)
*Ethan alone. Vanessa stands over him with a pill cup.*

**VANESSA:** Take your medicine, babe.
*Ethan looks at the pills, then at her. He takes them. When she leaves, he spits them into his
hand and pushes them deep into the mattress.*

**ETHAN** *(V.O.)*: Grace taught me that. Hold the pills under the tongue. Count to ten.

**58. GUEST ROOM — NIGHT** (25:55)
*Bradley and Vanessa think he's asleep.*

**BRADLEY:** The transfer to Ridgeview is set for Thursday, 7 a.m. The hearing's Friday. By Monday,
Atlas wires the money.
**VANESSA:** And by Christmas, nobody will remember Ethan Caldwell existed.

*The camera slowly finds the red light of the hidden recorder behind the Christmas boxes.*

## PART 6 — THE RESCUE (27:00–31:00)

**59. GRACE'S APARTMENT — DAY** (27:00)
*A knock. Harold stands at the door with a laptop.*

**HAROLD:** Miss Miller. I'm sorry it took this long. I had to be sure.
*He presses play. Vanessa's voice:*
**VANESSA** *(recording)*: "…put the bracelet in her bag, call the cops, cry a little, mention the
brother. She's gone by the weekend, and nobody's left to tell the judge he's improving…"
**BRADLEY** *(recording)*: "And the pills. If he'd taken the whole bottle at the party like I planned,
we wouldn't need any of this."

*Grace covers her mouth.*

**HAROLD:** Attempted murder, fraud, a false police report. But here's the problem. They move him to
Memphis Thursday morning. Once he's in Ridgeview, they control his doctors, his medicine, and his
visitors. I've filed for an emergency order, but the judge can't sign it before 6 a.m.
**GRACE:** Then we stop that van.
**HAROLD** *(smiling slowly)*: Ethan said you'd say that.

**60. BELLE MEADE BOULEVARD — THURSDAY, 6:30 A.M.** (28:10)
*Fog. A white transport van idles at the end of the street. Grace sits in Denise's old Honda.
DENISE, the fired nurse, is at the wheel.*

**DENISE:** When you called, I didn't even ask. I just said "what time."
**GRACE** *(checking her phone)*: Judge signed at 6:15. Harold's ten minutes out.
**DENISE:** That van isn't waiting ten minutes.

**61. MANSION DRIVEWAY — 6:41 A.M.** (28:35)
*The van pulls in. Two attendants get out with a stretcher. Vanessa opens the door in a silk robe,
coffee in hand.*

*Grace jumps out of the car and runs up the driveway.*

**VANESSA:** Oh, you have GOT to be kidding me. Get off my property or I'm calling the police.
**GRACE:** Please do. I'd love them here for this.
**VANESSA** *(to attendants)*: Ignore her. She's a thief. Go get him.

*Mrs. Hayes steps into the doorway, blocking the attendants.*

**MRS. HAYES:** Nobody goes through this door.
**VANESSA:** You're FIRED!
**MRS. HAYES:** Honey, I quit thirty seconds ago.

**62. FOYER — CONTINUOUS** (29:15)
**GRACE** *(to attendants)*: That man is not incompetent. He can talk, he can think, and he does not
want to go with you.
**VANESSA:** He can't even stand up!

*From down the hallway comes a sound: the scrape of a wheelchair, and something heavy hitting the floor.*

*Everyone turns.*

**63. HALLWAY — CONTINUOUS** (29:35)
*Ethan has pulled himself up on the doorframe. His legs shake violently, but he's standing.*

**ETHAN:** My name… is Ethan… Caldwell. And I'm… not going… ANYWHERE.

*Vanessa's coffee cup shatters on the marble.*

**64. DRIVEWAY — CONTINUOUS** (30:00)
*Harold's car comes through the fog with a police cruiser behind it. Officer Reyes steps out.*

**HAROLD** *(handing over the order)*: Emergency protective order. Mr. Caldwell goes to a facility of
HIS choosing. And Officer, you'll want to hear a recording about a certain bracelet.

*Reyes listens to ten seconds of audio on Harold's phone. He takes off his hat and walks to Grace.*

**REYES:** Ma'am… I owe you an apology.

**65. DENISE'S CAR — MOMENTS LATER** (30:35)
*Grace and Denise help Ethan into the back seat. Grace slides in beside him. As the car pulls
away, Ethan reaches over and laces his fingers through hers.*

*Vanessa watches from the doorway in her robe, frozen. Bradley is behind her, pale.*

## PART 7 — "I HEARD YOU" (31:00–34:00)

**66. REHAB CENTER, CHATTANOOGA — MONTAGE** (31:00)
*Weeks pass. Ethan at real parallel bars. Denise coaching. Grace cheering from a bench with a
thermos of black coffee with one ice cube. One step. Two. Ten.*

**67. REHAB GYM — DAY** (31:30)
**ETHAN** *(gritting his teeth)*: If you tell me one more Titans score, I'm going to fall on purpose.
**GRACE:** They won last week.
**ETHAN** *(stopping)*: …They did?
**GRACE:** No. But you just walked twelve steps without noticing.

*He looks down at his feet, then at her, and laughs.*

**68. REHAB GARDEN — NIGHT** (32:00)
*String lights. A bench. Ethan, now with a cane, sits beside Grace.*

**ETHAN:** That night in the storage room. "I think I'm falling in love with you, and I don't know
which one scares me more."
*Grace freezes.*
**GRACE:** You… heard that.
**ETHAN:** Grace, I heard every word you said to me for two months. It's the only reason I kept
fighting.
**GRACE:** Ethan, I was tired, I didn't mean—
**ETHAN:** Did you mean it?
*Long pause.*
**GRACE** *(whisper)*: Yes.

*He leans in. She leans in.*

*Then she stops, a breath away.*

**GRACE:** You're still engaged to her. I won't be what she called me in front of that whole street.
Not even for you.
**ETHAN** *(nodding slowly)*: Then I'd better fix that.

**69. REHAB ROOM — NEXT MORNING** (33:00)
*Ethan on the phone.*

**ETHAN:** Harold. Call off the engagement. Officially. And find out when the board votes on the Atlas
sale.
**HAROLD** *(phone)*: Monday, 10 a.m. Bradley's presenting.
**ETHAN:** Good. I'll be there.

## PART 8 — THE BOARDROOM (34:00–37:30)

**70. CALDWELL FREIGHT LOBBY — MONDAY** (34:00)
*Ethan walks in with a cane, in a navy suit. Grace is beside him in a simple blue dress. Harold
follows. Employees stop and stare.*

**EMPLOYEE 1:** That's Mr. Caldwell!
**EMPLOYEE 2:** He's walking!
**GRACE** *(quietly)*: Everyone's watching.
**ETHAN:** Let them. I already lost everything I was afraid to lose. Except you.

**71. BOARDROOM — CONTINUOUS** (34:30)
*Bradley at the head of the table. Vanessa beside him in red. Atlas executives with contracts. The
doors open.*

**BRADLEY** *(going white)*: Ethan. You… you should be resting.
**ETHAN:** I've rested enough. I believe you're in my chair.

*Bradley slowly stands. Ethan sits.*

**72. BOARDROOM — CONTINUOUS** (35:00)
**ETHAN:** I understand decisions have been made without me.
**BRADLEY:** We were protecting the company.
**ETHAN:** From me?
*(to Harold)* Play it.

*The recording fills the room: the pills, the bracelet, Ridgeview, Rick. Board members turn,
one by one, to stare at Bradley and Vanessa.*

**73. BOARDROOM — CONTINUOUS** (35:40)
**VANESSA:** Ethan, baby, that was taken out of context, Bradley forced me—
**BRADLEY:** I forced YOU? It was your idea!
**ETHAN:** Enough. *(to the board)* I move to cancel the Atlas sale and remove Bradley Caldwell as

**BOARD MEMBER:** Seconded.
**ETHAN:** All in favor?
*Every hand goes up.*

**74. BOARDROOM DOORS — CONTINUOUS** (36:10)

**DETECTIVE:** Bradley Caldwell, Vanessa Pierce, you're under arrest for attempted murder, fraud, and
filing a false police report.
*Handcuffs. Vanessa turns to Ethan as she's led out.*
**VANESSA:** Ethan, please. I loved you.
**ETHAN:** No. You loved my life. She loved me when I didn't have one.

*Vanessa looks at Grace. Grace looks right back. She doesn't look at the floor anymore.*

**75. LOBBY — DAY** (36:50)
*Employees applaud as Ethan walks out. Grace takes his free arm.*

**GRACE:** How did it go?
**ETHAN:** I think you already know.

## PART 9 — THE PROPOSAL (37:30–40:30)

**76. GRACE'S APARTMENT — DAY** (37:30)
*Grace opens an email. She screams.*

**GRACE:** MAMA! SAM! I PASSED! I'm a licensed physical therapist!
*Mama Ruth and Sam pile onto her. Ethan watches from the doorway with flowers, grinning.*
**ETHAN:** I'm proud of you.
**GRACE:** You knew?
**ETHAN:** Harold has friends at the licensing board. *(beat)* Kidding. I just refreshed the website
forty times.

**77. MANSION PORCH — NIGHT** (38:05)
*Candles everywhere, string lights, Johnny Cash playing softly. Ethan, no cane, stands by a table
for two. He hands Grace a small gift wrapped in brown paper.*

**GRACE:** What's this?
**ETHAN:** My mother's gardening gloves. Harold kept something in his safe for ten years and told me
to give it to the woman I wanted to marry.

*She slides her hand into one glove and feels something in the ring finger. A gold ring falls into
her palm, along with a folded, yellowed note.*

**78. PORCH — CONTINUOUS** (38:40)
*Grace reads it aloud, voice shaking.*

**GRACE:** "Ethan. If you're reading this, Harold says you finally found a woman who loves you and not
your money. Took you long enough. Marry her. Love, Mom."

*She looks up. Ethan is slowly getting down on one knee, without the cane.*

**ETHAN:** When I had everything, I thought I knew what love looked like. Then I lost everything, and
you stayed. Grace Miller, will you stay for good?
**GRACE** *(crying, laughing)*: Ethan Caldwell… yes. Yes!

*He slides the ring on. They kiss, their first, under the string lights.*

**79. MANSION GARDEN — WEDDING DAY** (39:25)
*A big outdoor wedding. Mama Ruth in a giant church hat is crying. Sam is the ring bearer in a tiny
tux. Mrs. Hayes is maid of honor. Denise and Harold are at the front. Officer Reyes is a guest.*

**GRACE** *(V.O.)*: My mama told me to be careful with my heart. I was. I gave it to the one man who
knew what it cost.

*Ethan and Grace share their first dance to "I Walk the Line." He spins her without a single stumble.*

**80. GARDEN — CONTINUOUS** (40:05)
*Two coffee cups on the porch rail. One is black, with one ice cube.*

**END CARD:** *"Some people love the life you have. The right one loves you when you have nothing."*
