**Goal:** a @rourke-level feed starring **you**. Every cover puts your real face, with your black glasses, into a creative AI scene, with big lime text layered in.
**Formats alternate:** 🎬 **AI Video** (cinematic Reel) · 🗣️ **UGC** (you talking to camera) · 🖼️ **Static** (carousel or single image)

Cover concepts come from `thumbnail-playbook.md`. Each one is generated in Higgsfield with your `okoye` element.

## How each format gets made

| Format | Made with | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 🎬 **AI Video** | Higgsfield image of you → animated with Kling 3.0 (start frame) → edit with voiceover and captions in CapCut | 15–30s Reel. Hook in the first 2 seconds. Cover = the strongest frame plus lime text. |
| 🗣️ **UGC** | Higgsfield talking-head clip of you **or** a real phone recording of you (more authentic) | AI UGC works for short hooks. Real footage builds the most trust. |
| 🖼️ **Static** | Higgsfield cover image of you + carousel slides from the `design/` templates | 7–9 slides, 4:5. Cover slide uses the photo style; inner slides use the dark lime-branded template. |

**Every cover gets:**
- your `okoye` face
- black glasses
- lime `#C6FF00` headline with a glow
- the feature word as an echo stack ×3
- the text behind your head where possible
