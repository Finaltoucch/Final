## New rule from Part 5: over-the-shoulder dialogue
Every two-person dialogue shot is framed over the listener's shoulder: the speaker sharp and facing camera, the listener's back of head and shoulder soft in the foreground. Kling prompt adds "only the person facing the camera speaks; the blurred person in the foreground stays still and silent" so lip sync stays on the speaker.
