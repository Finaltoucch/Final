# TWO BOYS, ONE HEART
### Full screenplay (about 15 minutes)

**Setting:** Atlanta, Georgia. Eastbrook High, a huge, prestigious suburban high school (big red-brick main building with a clock tower, its own stadium and gym), senior year, spring.
**Hook:** Seedance 2.5. **Everything else:** Kling 3.0 pro, over-the-shoulder dialogue.
**Music:** original score only (`tools/score.py`). No licensed songs at prom, or anywhere else.

---

## CAST

| Character | Who they are |
|---|---|
| **ZARA** (18) | Beautiful, warm, a little stubborn. Sings in the choir and wants to be a doctor. Raised by her grandma. |
| **MALIK** (18) | Poor, gentle, handsome, top of the class. Works weekends at his uncle's car wash. Says more in notes than out loud. |
| **TREY** (18) | Rich, bold, handsome, a little arrogant. Basketball star who drives to school every morning in a red convertible sports car, top down. Lonely underneath. |
| **GRANDMA PEARL** (70s) | Zara's grandma. Funny, sharp, brushes Zara's long hair at the kitchen table. |
| **BRIANNA** (18) | Zara's best friend. Loud and loyal. Secretly likes Trey. |
| **MS. ANGELA** (40s) | Malik's mom. Works two jobs. Tired, proud, soft with her son. |
| **MR. WHITFIELD** (50s) | Trey's father. Property developer. Cold, busy, never there. |

---

## HOOK: THE PEP RALLY (0:00–0:45)

**INT. SCHOOL GYM. DAY.**

The bleachers are packed and loud. Students stamp their feet, and the school colours hang everywhere.

ZARA sits halfway up the bleachers next to BRIANNA, laughing, until the lights dim.

Four of TREY's teammates run onto the court and unroll a giant hand-painted banner:

**"ZARA, PROM?"**

The gym explodes.

TREY walks across the court in his letterman jacket, a dozen red roses in his hand, grinning like he already knows the answer. He stops at the bottom of the bleachers and looks up at her.

> **TREY**
> So? You gonna make me stand here all day, or what?

The crowd screams. Brianna grabs Zara's arm.

> **BRIANNA**
> Girl. Girl!

Zara can't breathe. Slowly she turns her head and looks back over her shoulder.

Three rows behind her, MALIK stands at the end of the row in his plain grey hoodie. In his hand is a folded card, hand-drawn in blue ink. He's been holding it for a while.

Their eyes meet.

Malik folds the card once more, slides it into his hoodie pocket, and turns away into the crowd.

Zara looks at Trey. Then at the place where Malik was. Then back at Trey.

**FREEZE.**

**TITLE CARD: TWO BOYS, ONE HEART**

**CARD: SIX WEEKS EARLIER**

## SCENE 1: THE TEST (0:45–1:45)

**EXT. EASTBROOK HIGH, PARKING LOT. MORNING.**

A candy-red convertible sports car rolls into the front row, top down. Heads turn. TREY steps out in his uniform, blazer open, tie loose, sunglasses pushed up on his head. Two girls wave; he winks. Across the lot, ZARA walks past with her backpack and rolls her eyes, but she looks a second too long.

Behind the red car, MALIK pedals in on a clean, shiny blue bicycle he obviously takes good care of, his uniform neat and pressed. He has to brake hard as the convertible swings into its spot. He doesn't complain. He just walks the bike to the rack and locks it.

**INT. CALCULUS CLASSROOM. DAY.**

The teacher hands back tests. Zara turns hers over: a red **D**. She quickly folds it in half and slides it under her notebook.

Behind her, Malik leans forward a little.

> **MALIK**
> Question four got me too.

> **ZARA**
> (without turning around)
> You got an A. I saw it.

> **MALIK**
> Yeah. But it hurt.

She turns around. He's smiling, just a little. She can't help smiling back.

> **ZARA**
> I need a B to keep my spot in the summer program at the hospital. If I don't get it, my grandma's gonna act like she's not disappointed, and that's worse.

Malik thinks for a second.

> **MALIK**
> I'm at the library Tuesdays and Thursdays after school. If you want. No pressure.

> **ZARA**
> You tutor people?

> **MALIK**
> Not really. I just explain stuff to myself out loud. You'd just be listening.

Zara laughs and turns back around. Malik looks down at his desk, trying not to smile too much.

---

## SCENE 2: THE LIBRARY (1:45–3:00)

**INT. PUBLIC LIBRARY. LATE AFTERNOON.** *(a short montage)*

**Tuesday.** Malik draws a graph on a scrap of paper. Zara frowns at it. He draws it again, slower.

**Thursday.** She gets one right. She throws both hands up. The librarian shushes her. Malik hides his laugh in his sleeve.

**The next Tuesday.** Zara opens her calculator case and finds a small folded note. In blue ink:

*"You're smarter than this test."*

She looks over. Malik is suddenly very busy with his textbook.

**Later.** Zara unwraps a sandwich and starts peeling off the crust, piece by piece. Without a word, Malik slides half of his own sandwich across the table. The crust is already cut off.

> **ZARA**
> How do you know I don't eat the crust?

> **MALIK**
> You've been peeling it off for three weeks. It's kind of loud.

> **ZARA**
> It's not loud.

> **MALIK**
> It's a little loud.

She throws a balled-up napkin at him. He catches it. They both laugh, quietly, so they don't get shushed again.

---

## SCENE 3: THE RIDE (3:00–4:10)

**EXT. NEIGHBOURHOOD STREET. AFTERNOON. HEAVY RAIN.**

Zara walks home with her backpack over her head. She's soaked.

Trey's red convertible slows down beside her, the black soft-top closed against the rain. The window rolls down. Trey.

> **TREY**
> Get in. You're getting soaked.

> **ZARA**
> I'm good.

> **TREY**
> You're literally dripping. Come on. I don't bite. Usually.

She hesitates. The rain gets heavier. She gets in.

**INT. TREY'S CAR. CONTINUOUS.**

Warm leather seats. Rain on the windshield. Trey turns up the heater without being asked.

> **TREY**
> You sing, right? Choir. Front row, left side.

> **ZARA**
> You noticed that?

> **TREY**
> I notice a lot. I just don't say it to everybody.

> **ZARA**
> You say everything to everybody, Trey.

> **TREY**
> (laughing)
> Okay, that's fair.

He pulls up outside her small house. She opens the door, then stops.

> **ZARA**
> Thank you. For real.

> **TREY**
> You're welcome. For real.

She runs inside. Trey sits there a moment longer, watching her front door, before he drives off.

---

## SCENE 4: GRANDMA'S KITCHEN (4:10–5:15)

**INT. GRANDMA PEARL'S KITCHEN. NIGHT.**

Zara sits on a low stool. GRANDMA PEARL sits behind her in a chair, slowly brushing her long straight hair. A pot simmers on the stove.

> **GRANDMA PEARL**
> So which one gave you that look on your face?

> **ZARA**
> What look?

> **GRANDMA PEARL**
> That one. The one I had in nineteen seventy-four.

Zara laughs and covers her face.

> **ZARA**
> There's this boy, Malik. He helps me with calculus. He's sweet, Grandma. Like, quiet sweet. He notices everything.

> **GRANDMA PEARL**
> Mm-hm.

> **ZARA**
> And then there's Trey. He's loud, and he's kind of full of himself. But when he looks at me, it's like nobody else is in the room.

Grandma Pearl sets down the brush and rests her hands on Zara's shoulders.

> **GRANDMA PEARL**
> Baby, listen to me. Don't pick the boy who makes you feel lucky. Pick the one who makes you feel like yourself.

Zara is quiet. She doesn't know the answer yet.

---

## SCENE 5: THE CAR WASH (5:15–6:15)

**EXT. SMALL CAR WASH. SATURDAY. SUNNY.**

Malik, soaked and in a work shirt, scrubs a muddy truck. Zara walks up holding two lemonades.

He looks down at his wet shirt, embarrassed.

> **MALIK**
> You were not supposed to see me like this.

> **ZARA**
> Like what? Working?

She hands him a lemonade. They sit on an overturned bucket and a step.

> **ZARA**
> You're here every weekend?

> **MALIK**
> Every weekend. My mom does mornings at the hospital laundry, and nights at the diner. I do Saturdays and Sundays. Between the two of us, the lights stay on.

> **ZARA**
> You never told me any of that.

> **MALIK**
> You never asked. And it's not a sad story. It's just my story.

Zara looks at him differently now, with something deeper than a crush.

---

## SCENE 6: THE EMPTY SEAT (6:15–7:15)

**INT. SCHOOL GYM. NIGHT. AFTER A BASKETBALL GAME.**

The gym is almost empty. The scoreboard still glows: **HOME 78, AWAY 61.**

Trey sits alone on the bottom bleacher, still in his jersey, looking up at one empty seat in the front row.

Zara walks over and sits down next to him.

> **ZARA**
> Thirty-one points. Everybody's talking about you.

> **TREY**
> Not everybody.

He nods at the empty seat.

> **TREY**
> My dad's seat. He's never sat in it. Not once. Four years.

> **ZARA**
> Trey.

> **TREY**
> He sends money. That's his way of saying he was there.

> **ZARA**
> That's not the same thing.

> **TREY**
> (quietly)
> Yeah. I know.

For the first time, he isn't cocky. He's just a boy who wanted his dad to show up. Zara puts her hand on his arm. He looks at her hand, then at her.

---

## SCENE 7: THE LOCKERS (7:15–7:50)

**INT. SCHOOL HALLWAY. DAY.**

Brianna leans on the locker next to Zara's.

> **BRIANNA**
> Trey Whitfield sat with you after the game? Girl, are you serious right now?

> **ZARA**
> It's not like that. And Malik is,

> **BRIANNA**
> Malik is nice. Malik is real nice. But nice doesn't pick you up in the rain.

Zara shuts her locker and walks off. Brianna watches Trey walk past at the end of the hall, and for a second her face falls. She likes him. Nobody knows.

---

## SCENE 8: THE CARD (7:50–8:30)

**INT. MALIK'S SMALL APARTMENT. NIGHT.**

At the kitchen table, Malik draws carefully in blue ink on a folded card: two stick figures on a library table, a calculator between them.

MS. ANGELA comes in from her shift, still in her diner apron, and looks over his shoulder.

> **MS. ANGELA**
> Just ask her, baby.

> **MALIK**
> Ask her to what? Ride on my handlebars to prom?

> **MS. ANGELA**
> She's not looking at your shoes, Malik. She's looking at you.

Malik looks down at the card and keeps drawing.

---

## SCENE 9: THE PEP RALLY (8:30–9:10)

**INT. SCHOOL GYM. DAY.**

*(The hook scene in full context. Reuse the hook shots, then continue.)*

The banner. The roses. "So? You gonna make me stand here all day, or what?"

Zara looks back. Malik puts the card away and walks off.

Zara turns back to Trey.

> **ZARA**
> Can I think about it?

The crowd groans. Trey just smiles. He isn't hurt; if anything he looks more sure.

> **TREY**
> Take your time. I'm not going anywhere.

The crowd cheers again. Zara keeps looking toward the doors Malik left through.

---

## SCENE 10: THE NOTICE (9:10–9:50)

**INT. MALIK'S APARTMENT BUILDING HALLWAY. EVENING.**

Malik comes home. Every door in the hallway has a white paper taped to it. So does his.

**INT. MALIK'S APARTMENT. CONTINUOUS.**

Ms. Angela sits at the table holding the notice. She has been crying and tries to hide it.

> **MALIK**
> Mama. What is this?

> **MS. ANGELA**
> The building got sold. We have sixty days.

Malik takes the paper and reads the bottom of the page: **WHITFIELD PROPERTIES.**

His jaw tightens.

---

## SCENE 11: THE PARKING LOT (9:50–10:40)

**EXT. SCHOOL PARKING LOT. MORNING.**

Trey leans on his red convertible, top down, laughing with friends. Malik leans his blue bicycle against a tree and walks straight up to him and pushes the notice into his chest.

> **MALIK**
> Sixty days. That's what your family gave mine.

> **TREY**
> What are you talking about?

> **MALIK**
> Read it. Read the name at the bottom.

Trey reads it. His face changes.

> **TREY**
> Man, I didn't know about this. I don't run my dad's company.

> **MALIK**
> No. You just drive it to school.

Trey steps forward. Malik doesn't step back. They're chest to chest.

Zara runs between them and pushes them apart, one hand on each chest.

> **ZARA**
> Stop! Both of you, stop.

Silence. Malik looks at Zara standing beside Trey's car. He steps back.

---

## SCENE 12: "NOT MINE" (10:40–11:30)

**EXT. EMPTY BLEACHERS BY THE FOOTBALL FIELD. DAY.**

Zara finds Malik sitting alone.

> **ZARA**
> Malik. Talk to me.

> **MALIK**
> What is there to say? He can give you everything. I can barely give my mom rent money.

> **ZARA**
> Did I ever ask you for anything?

> **MALIK**
> No. You never did. That's what makes it worse.

He stands up.

> **MALIK**
> You belong in his world, Zara. Not mine.

> **ZARA**
> Don't tell me where I belong.

> **MALIK**
> (softly)
> I'm not trying to hurt you. I'm trying to stop hurting myself.

He walks away down the bleacher steps. Zara stands alone in the wind.

**INT. CHOIR ROOM. LATER.**

Zara sits on the floor behind the piano, crying. The door opens. Brianna sits down next to her.

> **BRIANNA**
> I'm sorry. I kept pushing you toward Trey because I liked him. I should have told you.

> **ZARA**
> (half laughing, half crying)
> Of course you did.

Brianna pulls her into a hug.

---

## SCENE 13: FATHER AND SON (11:30–12:30)

**INT. WHITFIELD HOUSE. HOME OFFICE. NIGHT.**

A big, cold room. MR. WHITFIELD is on his laptop. Trey walks in through the wooden door and puts the notice on the desk.

> **TREY**
> The Eastside building. People live there, Dad. A kid from my school lives there.

> **MR. WHITFIELD**
> (not looking up)
> It's business, son. You'll understand one day.

> **TREY**
> You never came to one game. Not one. Four years, and that seat was always empty.

Mr. Whitfield finally looks up.

> **TREY**
> I stopped asking you to show up for me. But don't take their home too.

> **MR. WHITFIELD**
> Watch your tone in my house.

Trey takes his car keys out of his pocket and sets them on the desk.

> **TREY**
> Keep the car. Keep all of it. Just stop the deal.

He walks out. Mr. Whitfield stares at the keys for a long time.

---

## SCENE 14: THE LETTER (12:30–12:55)

**INT. MALIK'S APARTMENT. MORNING.**

Ms. Angela opens a new letter and reads it twice. Her hand goes to her mouth.

> **MS. ANGELA**
> They pulled out. The sale's off. We can stay.

> **MALIK**
> What? Why?

> **MS. ANGELA**
> It doesn't say. It just says we can stay.

She hugs him hard. Over her shoulder, Malik looks confused. He has no idea who did this.

---

## SCENE 15: PROM NIGHT, GRANDMA'S HOUSE (12:55–13:20)

**INT. GRANDMA PEARL'S LIVING ROOM. EVENING.**

Zara stands in a simple, beautiful gold dress. Grandma Pearl fixes one last curl.

> **GRANDMA PEARL**
> So. Which one?

> **ZARA**
> I don't know, Grandma.

> **GRANDMA PEARL**
> Yes, you do. You just don't want to hurt the other one.

Zara looks at herself in the mirror. Her eyes are wet.

---

## SCENE 16: PROM (13:20–14:35)

**INT. SCHOOL GYM, DECORATED FOR PROM. NIGHT.**

Lights, gold balloons, couples dancing.

Trey walks in through the gym doors in a black suit. Zara spots him.

> **ZARA**
> I didn't hear your car.

> **TREY**
> I took a ride share. Long story.

Zara looks at him for a long moment. She puts it together.

> **ZARA**
> It was you. Malik's building.

Trey doesn't answer. Then, quietly:

> **TREY**
> Don't tell him. He'd hate owing me.

Zara's eyes fill with tears. She's about to speak when the doors open again.

Malik. A borrowed suit, a little too big in the shoulders, and in his hand the hand-drawn card from the pep rally, a little bent now.

He walks up to Zara. Trey steps back half a step.

> **MALIK**
> I should have given you this six weeks ago.

She opens it. Inside, in blue ink:

*"I'm not good with words out loud. So here. I'd rather fail calculus with you than pass it with anyone else."*

Zara laughs and cries at the same time. She looks up at Malik, then turns to Trey.

Trey already knows. He smiles, the real one, not the cocky one.

> **TREY**
> I know. I saw your face when he walked in.

> **ZARA**
> Trey, I,

> **TREY**
> Don't. You don't owe me a speech.

He turns to Malik and holds out his hand. Malik looks at it, then shakes it.

> **TREY**
> She's worth it. Make sure she knows it.

> **MALIK**
> Every day.

Trey walks toward the exit. Malik offers Zara his hand.

> **MALIK**
> I can't really dance.

> **ZARA**
> I know. It's kind of loud.

He laughs. They dance, badly and happily, in the middle of the floor.

---

## SCENE 17: OUTSIDE (14:35–14:55)

**EXT. SCHOOL ENTRANCE. NIGHT.**

Trey walks out alone into the night, hands in his pockets.

A dark car is parked at the curb. Mr. Whitfield is leaning against it. Trey stops.

> **MR. WHITFIELD**
> I cancelled the deal.

Trey doesn't move.

> **MR. WHITFIELD**
> And I watched the tape of your game. All of it. Thirty-one points.
> (his voice breaks)
> I should have been in that seat, son.

Trey's face crumbles. He walks into his father's arms. His dad holds him tightly, for the first time in years.

---

## CLOSING CARDS (14:55–15:10)

**CARD:** *"Love isn't about who can give you the most. It's about who makes you feel like yourself."*

**CARD:** *"Who would you have chosen?"*

**THE END**

## Production notes

- **Hook (Seedance):** a crowded gym, the banner, the roses walk, Zara's look back, Malik pocketing the card. Trey's single hook line can be a Kling over-the-shoulder insert if Seedance's lip sync isn't clean. Scene 9 reuses these shots.
- **Lookalikes:** Malik and Trey must look completely different side by side:
  - **Malik:** slimmer, close low fade, no facial hair, grey hoodie, warm brown skin.
  - **Trey:** taller and broader, high-top fade with sharp line-up, thin mustache, letterman jacket, deeper brown skin.
  - **Zara:** long, sleek, bone-straight jet-black hair with a middle part in every scene (locked sheet `2f59b687…`), gold dress at prom.
  - No gym extra may share a lead's face.
- **Prop continuity:**
  - The blue-ink card: folded at the pep rally, slightly bent at prom.
  - The eviction notice: the same paper from Scene 10 to Scene 13.
  - Malik's bicycle: the same clean, new, glossy royal-blue bike (black saddle, silver bell and fenders, no basket) in every shot. Malik is always neat and clean.
  - Trey's car: the same candy-red convertible from the locked sheet `f295c3d4…` (sharp wedge body, slim LED light strips, gloss-black wheels, brown leather seats, no badge) in every shot, top down on dry days, black soft-top closed in the rain. His keys are gone after Scene 13, so no car from Scene 16 on.
- **School uniform:** navy blazer with the Eastbrook High crest on the left chest pocket (maroon shield, gold border, gold oak tree, open book, laurel, no letters), white shirt, maroon tie, grey skirt or trousers. Students wear it at school; weekend and prom scenes are in their own clothes.
- **No brands:** no car logos, no real school or college names, no sports logos.
- **Kling-safe lines:** no "..." and no CAPS inside lines. Lines that trail off ("Trey, I,") need "cut off mid-sentence" written into the prompt.
- **Score cues:** `hope` for the library, `tender` for the car wash, kitchen and prom dance, `sad` for the bleachers and choir room, `tension` for the notice and parking lot, `dark` for the father's office, and `tender` swelling into `hope` for the father's hug.
