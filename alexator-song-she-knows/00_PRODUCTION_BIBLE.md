# THE SONG SHE STILL KNOWS
ALEXATOR **019 — Never Let Go** · vertical 9:16 AI music video · 10 shots

> **Track note.** 019 Never Let Go has been used twice before in this engagement
> (WHERE THE SOUND IS, project 7; RAID, project 13). The client supplied it
> again on 13 October 2026 and was told of the repeat before the build started.

## CONCEPT
An old man visits his wife in a care home. She does not know who he is any more.
He plays their song on a small speaker, and on the drop her fingers start moving
in time and she hums every note of it. She still does not know his name. It does
not matter today. Register: **memory → the music reaching her.**

This is the one where the music is not a soundtrack. It is the thing that works.

## SUBJECTS — PASTE VERBATIM INTO EVERY PROMPT

**HENRY — the husband, 79.** A white man of seventy-nine, thin and stooped,
fine silver hair combed back flat, a deeply lined face, pale blue eyes, prominent
liver spots on the backs of both hands, and a loose gold wedding band on a thin
fourth finger of the left hand. WARDROBE, UNCHANGING: a pressed white shirt
buttoned to the top, a maroon tie, and a soft grey wool cardigan.

**ELSIE — his wife, 77.** A white woman of seventy-seven, soft white hair set in
short waves, pale papery skin, gentle unfocused eyes, hands resting loose.
WARDROBE, UNCHANGING: a lilac knitted cardigan over a cream blouse, and a pale
green wool blanket across her knees.

**WORLD.** A quiet care home day room. One tall window with white light, a
high-backed armchair, a low side table, pale walls. Warm, clean, not clinical.
Photoreal, 35 mm, shallow depth of field.

## RULES CARRIED IN
- No kissing anywhere (client standing rule).
- No grid, no collage.
- Shot 07 is an object insert: LOCKED-OFF camera, or the push deletes the
  speaker the way it deleted the candle and emptied the wine glass.
- Shot 07 still needs motion from a living subject in frame, or freezedetect
  fires at d=0.3 — keep her in shot, breathing.
- Elderly hands are the highest-risk element in this film. Inspect every hand in
  every keyframe at NATIVE RESOLUTION before ordering any clip.
- The framed photograph in shot 02 must stay abstract and out of focus — no
  reference to any real person, per the brief.
