# WHAT THE HANDS LET GO
**ALEXATOR — *Brighter Days* (008)**
Project 10. Register: **letting go.**

## Concept

A woman who rehabilitates injured wild birds carries a recovered kestrel in
a cloth-lined box through a wet forest at first light. She sets it down in a
clearing, kneels, lifts the bird out, and holds it against her chest a moment
longer than she needs to. Then she opens her hands. It is gone. She stays
kneeling with empty hands.

One person. One bird. Nothing supernatural, nothing impossible.

### Why this concept

Three earlier proposals were rejected, and each rejection sharpened this one:

1. **"ONE BREATH"** (freediving) — rejected as a repeat. Skydiving then
   freediving reads as the same idea twice, whatever the surface differences.
2. **"FIRST RAIN"** (weather as protagonist) — rejected: there must be a
   person or people.
3. **"THE LAUNCH"** (nine people pushing a boat) — rejected, and fairly. I
   had described crowds as the thing that breaks these films and then
   proposed nine people in the same message.

So: deliberately minimal. One person, one animal, one gesture. The whole film
turns on a pair of hands opening.

## Bird handling — risk mitigation

Animated birds in flight are the highest-risk thing in this film. Mitigations:

- The kestrel is in her hands or at rest for **nine of eleven shots**.
- **One** flight shot (08), and the bird goes *away* from camera into light —
  the angle that shows the least wing mechanics.
- Shot 07 is a single instant of take-off, not sustained flight.
- Her face (09), not the bird, carries the release.

## Character

Woman, about 55. Pale weathered skin, fine lines, freckles, grey-blue eyes,
dark brown hair heavily streaked with grey in a low knot. Dark olive waxed
cotton jacket, oatmeal wool jumper, dark brown canvas work trousers, scuffed
brown leather boots. Bare hands — no gloves, no jewellery, no logos.

## Location

Small clearing in wet temperate deciduous woodland at first light. Fixed
landmarks for continuity: mossy fallen trunk on the left, slender silver
birch with peeling white bark at the back, bracken and fern at the edges,
open leaf-litter patch in the centre. Hard low horizontal sun in shafts
through drifting mist. **Generated empty of people** so no crowd is baked in.

## The kestrel

Male common kestrel: blue-grey head, dark moustachial stripe, chestnut back
with black diamond flecks, cream underparts streaked dark brown, blue-grey
tail with one broad black band and a white tip, yellow legs with black
talons, blue-grey hooked bill with yellow cere, dark eye with yellow ring.
Healthy and uninjured throughout — no wounds, blood, missing feathers or
bandages, and no cage, wire, leash or straps in any frame.

## The box

Plain unpainted wooden carrying box, bare smooth blank wood, rope handle,
small brass hook-and-eye catch, lined with pale grey cloth.

Deliberately specified as **blank** in every prompt. Per the EXIT root-cause
note: the word "panel" invites a printed caption, and negatives do not
reliably suppress text. Remove the surface, or crop past it.
