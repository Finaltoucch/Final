# Finaltoucch — Channel Thumbnail Style Guide

**Channel:** youtube.com/@Finaltoucch
**Niche:** Upwork / freelancing / getting clients (+ AI-tools experiments)
**Purpose of this doc:** A reusable recipe so every new thumbnail (designed by hand
or generated with AI) stays 100% on-brand and instantly recognizable in the feed.

---

## 1. The Style DNA (what makes a Finaltoucch thumbnail)

Every high-performing thumbnail on the channel shares the same five ingredients:

1. **The face** — the creator, always. Glasses, suit/blazer, studio-lit,
   cut out cleanly against the background. The face IS the brand.
2. **A big exaggerated expression or gesture** that matches the video's emotion
   (shocked, thinking, taped mouth, money in mouth, pointing).
3. **Dark background + one glowing accent color** — usually near-black or deep
   green with a neon-green glow (Upwork brand green pulls double duty as the
   channel accent).
4. **2–5 giant words max**, stacked, in a heavy sans-serif — one word is almost
   always highlighted in a different color or boxed.
5. **One recognizable brand mark or prop** (Upwork logo, Slack logo, Contra logo,
   cash, a document, a red ❌) that tells the topic in half a second.

> Rule of thumb: if someone sees the thumbnail at phone size for 0.5 seconds,
> they should know (a) it's Finaltoucch and (b) roughly what the video is about.

---

## 2. Color palette

| Role | Color | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Background base | Near-black `#0A0F0A` / deep green `#0B2B13` | Full-bleed dark canvas, subtle radial glow behind subject |
| Primary accent | Upwork neon green `#14A800` → glow `#6FDA44` | Keyword text, logo, rim-light/glow effects |
| Text base | Pure white `#FFFFFF` | Main headline words |
| Alarm accent | Red `#E23B3B` | "MISTAKES", ❌ marks, warning boxes, strike-throughs |
| Money accent | Gold/yellow `#F5C542` | Occasional highlight word ("MONEY") or cash props |
| Alt background | Deep purple `#2B1B4D` | Used when the topic isn't Upwork (e.g. Slack video) so green stays exclusive to Upwork content |

**Rules:**
- Maximum **one glow color per thumbnail**. Green for Upwork topics, purple/other
  for non-Upwork topics, so the green stays a topic signal.
- Red appears only as a *negative* signal (mistakes, ignored proposals, ❌).
- Backgrounds are never busy — dark, clean, with a soft glow vignette behind
  the face and the text.

## 3. Layout grid

Canvas: **1280×720**. Mentally split it into two columns:

```
┌───────────────────────────┬──────────────┐
│  TEXT / LOGO ZONE (~60%)  │  FACE ZONE   │
│                           │   (~40%)     │
│  Big stacked words,       │  Chest-up    │
│  brand logo above or      │  cutout,     │
│  inside the text block    │  facing      │
│                           │  inward      │
└───────────────────────────┴──────────────┘
```

- **Face right, text left** is the default (matches most uploads).
  Flipping it is fine, but keep the face looking *toward* the text.
- The face can also go **center** when the expression itself is the story
  (e.g. money-in-mouth "Hidden Secrets" thumbnail) — then text wraps
  above/beside it.
- **Bottom-left corner stays clear-ish** — YouTube stamps the duration
  bottom-right; keep critical words out of the extreme corners.
- Props overlap the text block slightly (logo tucked behind a word, cash
  breaking the text edge) for depth — flat layers look cheap.

---

## 4. Typography

- **Font family:** ultra-bold condensed sans-serif (Anton, Archivo Black,
  Bebas Neue Bold, or similar). ALL CAPS, always.
- **Word count:** 2–5 words. Never a full sentence — the *title* carries the
  sentence, the thumbnail carries the punch.
- **Hierarchy trick (signature move):** one word gets special treatment —
  - different color (green or red), OR
  - a filled box behind it (red box on "MISTAKES"), OR
  - dramatically larger size ("MONEY", "ACCOUNT").
- **Stacking:** words stack vertically, left-aligned or centered — not a
  single long line.
- Subtle outer glow or drop shadow on text so it pops off the dark background;
  no thick cartoon outlines.

---

## 5. Face & expression rules

- Wardrobe: glasses + dark suit/blazer (white or black shirt). Consistent
  wardrobe = channel recognition.
- Lighting: clean studio key light + a rim light in the accent color
  (green edge-glow ties the cutout to the background).
- Expression menu — pick ONE that matches the video's promise:

| Video emotion | Expression / gesture |
|---|---|
| Tutorial / how-to | Confident neutral, slight smile, or thinking pose (hand on chin) |
| Secrets / insider info | Raised eyebrow, sideways glance, or a "shhh"-adjacent prop gag |
| Mistakes / warnings | Mouth taped (green tape!), grimace, or hands on cheeks (shock) |
| Money / results | Cash prop interaction — money in mouth, pointing at a payout card |
| Curiosity / surprise | Wide eyes + open mouth, hands framing the face |

- **Prop-gags are the channel's superpower** (taped mouth, money in mouth).
  Aim for one physical gag per 3–4 uploads so it stays special.

---

## 6. Topic badges & props

- **Upwork videos:** Upwork wordmark/logo, proposal documents, payout/contract
  UI cards ("Milestone 1: $3,500", "ACTIVE" tags), cash bundles.
- **Platform videos (Slack, Contra, etc.):** that platform's logo big and
  center-adjacent, background shifts away from green.
- **Proof elements:** small screenshot cards (chat replies, contract cards)
  tilted 3–8° so they read as "real evidence", with a glow border.
- **Negative markers:** big red ❌, red strike-through, red box — only on
  "mistakes / why you're failing" videos.

## 7. Per-genre recipes (paint-by-numbers)

**A. How-to / tutorial** (e.g. "How to Create an Upwork Account")
- BG: near-black + green glow
- Text: 3–4 stacked words, "FREE"/action word in green with a ✔ or badge
- Face: right side, confident
- Prop: platform logo inside text block

**B. Mistakes / warning** (e.g. "Upwork MISTAKES")
- BG: dark + green glow
- Text: topic word white, "MISTAKES" in a red box
- Face: gagged/taped mouth or grimace
- Prop: red ❌ near the corner

**C. Money / results** (e.g. "Slack = MONEY", "$10,000")
- BG: dark (purple if non-Upwork)
- Text: "MONEY"/dollar amount oversized in gold or green
- Face: interacting with cash, or thinking pose next to proof screenshot
- Prop: cash bundle or payout card breaking the text edge

**D. Secrets / insider** (e.g. "HIDDEN SECRETS", "contra SECRETS")
- BG: darkest of all, tight glow
- Text: "SECRETS" as the hero word (green or gold)
- Face: shocked or sly expression, center or right
- Prop: platform logo + one "evidence" card

**E. AI / experiment videos** (e.g. "PRO MOVIE AI")
- These may use a cinematic still as background instead of the dark canvas —
  the exception genre. Keep ONE stacked bold text block and, when possible,
  a small creator-face inset so the brand face never disappears from the feed.

## 8. AI-generation prompt template

For Higgsfield / other image models, use this base prompt and fill the slots:

```
YouTube thumbnail, 1280x720, ultra-clean professional design.
Background: near-black [#0A0F0A] with a soft neon-green radial glow.
Right side: chest-up photo cutout of a young Black man wearing glasses and a
dark suit, [EXPRESSION — e.g. "shocked, hands on cheeks"], studio lighting
with a green rim light, facing slightly left.
Left side: giant stacked ALL-CAPS ultra-bold condensed sans-serif text:
"[LINE 1]" in white, "[HERO WORD]" in neon green [#6FDA44]
(or in a solid red box for warning topics).
Prop: [Upwork logo / cash bundle / tilted screenshot card / red X].
High contrast, minimal elements, no clutter, text extremely legible at
small sizes, glossy modern YouTube style.
```

Slots to fill per video: `[EXPRESSION]`, `[LINE 1]`, `[HERO WORD]`, `[PROP]`,
and swap the green for purple `#7B4DFF` on non-Upwork platform videos.

---

## 9. Pre-publish checklist

- [ ] Readable at 320px wide (shrink it — can you read every word on a phone?)
- [ ] ≤ 5 words, one hero word treated differently
- [ ] One glow color only; red used only for negatives
- [ ] Face cutout clean (no halo/fringe), rim light matches the glow color
- [ ] Topic identifiable in 0.5s from logo/prop alone
- [ ] Nothing critical in the bottom-right corner (duration stamp)
- [ ] Thumbnail promise matches the title's promise (no bait mismatch)
- [ ] Side-by-side check against the last 3 uploads — consistent, but not identical

## 10. Pairing with the script docs in this repo

For the Bright Side–style scripts (`brightside-style-script.md`,
`brightside-what-if-script.md`), adapt the same DNA rather than copying
Bright Side's cartoon thumbnails:

- **"10 Body Signals" video:** dark canvas, giant green "10", creator's face
  in shocked pose, red circle on a body part, hero word "IGNORE" in a red box.
- **"Earth Stopped Spinning" video:** dark space canvas, Earth with a giant
  red PAUSE symbol, creator's small shocked face inset, text "JUST 5 SECONDS"
  with "5 SECONDS" in green.

That keeps a new content genre visually stitched to the existing channel brand.
