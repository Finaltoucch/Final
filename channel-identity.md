# Channel Identity Kit

## Channel name

**Stay Curious** — matches the banner/profile art and the sign-off line used in both
video scripts ("Stay curious — and enjoy the spin"). One clear promise, easy to say,
easy to remember, genre-accurate for pop-science listicles and What-If videos.

Backup names (if the handle is taken):
- The Curious Side
- Wonder Why
- Stay Curious TV
- Curiosity Club

## Handle

First choice: `@StayCurious` — fallbacks: `@StayCuriousShow`, `@StayCuriousTV`,
`@ItsStayCurious`.

fun, animated answers. New video every week. Stay curious." Logo sting.
