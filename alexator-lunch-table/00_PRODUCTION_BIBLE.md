# THE LUNCH TABLE
ALEXATOR **022 — Kindred Soul** · vertical 9:16 AI music video · 10 shots

## CONCEPT
A girl's first day at a new school. She eats lunch alone in a hall full of
noise. Nobody is cruel to her — she is simply invisible, which is worse. On the
drop a second tray lands opposite hers and somebody sits down.
Register: **invisible → seen.**

## SUBJECTS — PASTE VERBATIM INTO EVERY PROMPT

**ELLIE — the new girl, 12.** A white girl of twelve, pale fair skin with a
light scatter of freckles across the nose, straight light-brown hair in one long
plait over her right shoulder, round thin-rimmed wire glasses, a small round
face and a serious closed mouth. Build: small for her age, narrow shoulders.
WARDROBE, UNCHANGING: a navy school jumper over a white shirt with a
maroon-and-gold striped tie, worn slightly too neatly, and a new stiff navy
backpack.

**MAISIE — the other girl, 12.** A white girl of twelve, fair skin, wavy auburn
hair gathered back in a thick ponytail, a wide open face and a gap between her
front teeth. WARDROBE, UNCHANGING: the same navy jumper, white shirt and
maroon-and-gold striped tie, worn untidily with the tie loose, and a battered
clear water bottle covered in stickers.

**The two girls must read as instantly distinguishable:** Ellie is small, in
glasses, with a straight light-brown plait; Maisie is taller, no glasses, wavy
auburn ponytail. That difference carries the whole last shot.

**WORLD.** A large British secondary-school dining hall. High windows, grey
daylight, long pale laminate tables, moulded plastic chairs, the roar of three
hundred children. Photoreal, 35 mm, shallow depth of field.

  tables: compose them in perspective, never as a flat repeating pattern.

- Check WHERE subjects are, shot against shot — her table, her seat and the

### FOUR SHOTS FAILED INSPECTION — THREE SEPARATE LESSONS

**1. When the subject is absence, the model empties the whole frame.**
Shots 07 and 08 both came back with the dining hall deserted — bare tables,
dirty trays, lunch clearly over. Both prompts had leaned on emptiness ("alone",
"completely empty", "the ABSENCE of a person"), and the model generalised that
from the one chair to the entire room. That breaks the story: the hall must be
full and loud or she is not being ignored, she is just late.
**Rule: when a shot is about one absence, state explicitly that everything
around it is full, occupied and in motion.** Both re-rolls carry a CRITICAL
line to that effect and both came back correct.

**2. Wardrobe continuity has to be restated in every single prompt.**
Shot 10 lost both girls' navy jumpers — they came back in shirtsleeves, the
background uniform changed to maroon blazers, and both girls read about fifteen
rather than twelve. Shot 10 happens *seconds* after shot 09. The fix was to say
so in the prompt — "this happens seconds after the previous shot, nothing about
the clothing, the ages or the room may change" — plus restating the jumper and
the age as CRITICAL lines.

**3. Props invent brand labels unless you ban them by name.**
The water bottle in shot 09 came back with a printed commercial label and a
barcode. That is third-party material in a paid deliverable and it cannot ship.
Re-prompted as "PLAIN COLOURED STICKERS ONLY — no printed label, no brand name,
no barcode, no readable writing of any kind", which produced clean stars and
circles.

The seating rule carried over from THE GOOD SHIRT worked first time: every
seated shot was framed three-quarter with the table top and chair seat visible
as flat planes, and not one girl floated.
