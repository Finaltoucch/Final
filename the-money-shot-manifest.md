Applied with `atempo=1.05`, silence-padded to the scene starts above, mixed at
`volume=3.2`. Placement is deliberate — concatenating the blocks back to back
puts Ben's backstory over Gabby's drawer scene, which is the misalignment that
broke the first cut.

## What the corrections fixed

The six notes, and what happened to each:

1. **Photograph content visible where she holds it** — reshot as a face-only
   close-up with the photograph below the frame line. The intermediate version
   that turned the photo around to show a blank white back was rejected: a white
   rectangle held up to camera reads as an empty prop, not as a hidden picture.
   The fix is framing, not the prop.
2. **Gabby's mother must be Black** — six shots reshot (18, 19, 31, 32, 37, 39),
   prompt pinned to "an elderly frail Black woman in her sixties with grey hair".
3. **Car sale / key handover frame errors** — reshot with a single key into an
   open hand and the buyer's back to camera, removing the hand geometry that
   was breaking.
4. **ATM shot** — covered by the same simplification pass.
5. **Photograph content visible in the café** — same treatment as note 1.
6. **The child in the photograph must be Black** — slot 38 reshot with "a young
   Black child".

## Still open

Slots 15 and 16 carry the blank-white-back version. The photo content is hidden,
so the note is satisfied literally, but the white card looks like a prop error.
Both need reframing the way slots 7 and 14 were done — **36 credits**, blocked on
an empty balance.

## Known limitation

Every clip was generated with `sound: "off"`. There is no ambient bed under the
picture, which is what makes the quiet moments feel synthetic. Fixing it at
source means regenerating all 41 shots with `sound: "on"` at 24 credits each
(~984 credits). The cheap route is a free ambient bed per location laid under
the narration in CapCut.
