The grid was anchored on the measured kick transient at 75.0832 s, not on the
comb filter's nearest beat (75.1211, 38 ms late) and not on the −14 dB threshold
crossing (75.035, 48 ms early). Anchoring on the audible event is what put the
door on the drop.

shot 06 — her hand reaching for the handle. The music thins under the reach and
slams back as the door opens. That fell out of the grid rather than being
designed.
