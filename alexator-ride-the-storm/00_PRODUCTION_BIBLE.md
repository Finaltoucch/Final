# RIDE THE STORM
**ALEXATOR — *Ride the Storm* (014)**
Project 12. Register: **defiance.**

## Concept

One rider. One empty road. One storm.

She rides toward a black wall of weather on an empty two-lane road across a
flat plain. She stops in the middle of the road and looks up at it. Then she
opens the throttle and goes into it anyway, and comes out the far side into
open gold light.

That is the whole film. No second character, no props to track, no
choreography, no dialogue, no crowd.

### How this concept was arrived at

Five concepts were rejected before this one, and the rejections are worth
recording because they contain the actual lesson.

1. **THE HAND** (Iliad wrestling match) — "nonsense." Substituted an unarmed
   contest for the armed duel that was asked for. Clever, and wrong.
2. **THE TOP OF THE TOWER** (wind turbine technician) — "not creative."
3. **THE MIRROR** (salt flat, inverted frame) — "not creative."
4. **THE CAST** (Roman spear duel) — superseded.
5. **Roman sword duel** — built to spec, and the keyframes failed on
   renderability (see the error note in 02).

The pattern across the first eleven delivered films: person cycles, person
forges, person works, person rides a train, person crosses a desert, person
goes to a party, person at home, person skydives, person frees a bird, two
men wrestle, person climbs a tower. **The subject kept changing and the form
never moved.** Every film was one protagonist performing a modest activity in
a striking location, cut in slow 2–4 bar holds.

The brief for this one was explicit: *simpler*. So the answer was to stop
being clever and build the thing the track is literally named for.

## Why this is simpler to BUILD, not just to describe

This is the operative design decision, and it is a direct response to the
Roman duel failing:

- **The rider wears a full-face helmet with the visor down in every shot.**
  The character is locked by costume, not by face. Face drift, the single
  largest consistency risk in this engagement, is removed entirely.
- **One body.** No two-person contact, no interlocking limbs, no relative
  scale to maintain between two figures.
- **No weapons, no armour, no handled props.**
- **The motorcycle is specified plain** — no decals, no badges, no brand
  name, no number plate. Those surfaces are where lettering creeps in.
- **Two reference sheets instead of three** (rider+bike, road+storm), which
  is 26.00 for the image stage rather than 28.00.

## The light plan

The film is structured on a single lighting arc, which is what carries the
emotion without needing a face:

**warm gold → dim green-grey → black and silver → gold again**

Sun behind camera for shots 01–06, storm overhead for 07–09, inside the rain
for 10, out the far side for 11. Shot 11 mirrors shot 04's framing so the
ending rhymes with the opening.

## Character

A woman, athletic build. Matte black one-piece leather riding suit, scuffed
at knees and elbows, armoured at shoulders, elbows and knees. Matte charcoal
grey full-face helmet, **dark smoked visor down at all times**, plain shell
with no graphics. Black leather gauntlet gloves. Black leather boots.

## The motorcycle

Stripped-back naked roadster, matte dark grey and black. Round single
headlight, low flat bars, exposed engine and frame tubes, twin exhaust, slim
black seat, small round analogue dial. Road grime and scratches on the tank.
**No decals, no stripes, no graphics, no brand name, no badges, no plate.**

## The location

A single empty two-lane asphalt road running dead straight across a vast flat
plain of dry pale scrub. An enormous black-blue shelf cloud with a hard
leading edge and rain shafts beneath. Sun still out behind camera so the near
road is warm gold and the storm wall ahead is almost black. Generated
**completely empty of vehicles, people, buildings, poles, and signs.**

- 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920, 24 fps, MP4, stereo — yes
- 30–60 s — 45.17 s
- Hook in the first 1–2 s — shot 01 opens on the glove closing on the throttle
- Professionally chosen section, natural fade-in, smooth fade-out — yes
- No stock footage, no third-party visual material — everything generated
- No real-person references — the face is never visible
- No violence, weapons, sexual content, minors, drugs, alcohol, smoking,
  hate symbols, political or religious content, brands, logos, readable text
  or watermarks — negatives carried on every prompt
