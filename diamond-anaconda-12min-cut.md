# DIAMOND ANACONDA — 12 MINUTE CUT

Target: **720 seconds.** The 42-minute screenplay is the source; this is what
survives. All seven cast Elements are still used — nobody is cut, the small parts
just die faster.

---

## What goes

- **The 1974 camp** — bones, notebook, torn pages. The best worldbuilding in the
  script and the first thing to go, because it explains rather than advances.
- **The canopy attack and the drone** — the flooded crossing already gives the
  audience the creature. The drone was the team catching up; at 12 minutes there
  is no room to catch up twice. **Liam now dies on the far bank of the crossing.**
- **The carving chamber** — the tallies, "it's where it comes back to." Folded into
  one line in the cavern.
- **The 1974 radio voice** stays. It costs eight seconds and it is the whole island.

## What is untouchable

The cold open, the legend, the flooded crossing, the egg reveal, Maya's sacrifice,
the cold-open resolve, and the final shot. Cut anything else first.
