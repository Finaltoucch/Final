## Standing caveat

The production environment cannot reach the Higgsfield CDN, so no frame of this film was ever
viewed during production. Every quality claim above is **measured** — dimensions, frame rate,
frame counts, duration, loudness, true peak, black/freeze detection, scene-change cut accuracy,
per-channel colour ratios — never watched. Visual approval was the client's throughout, which is
why keyframes were shown for sign-off before any clip budget was spent.
