## Note on prop consistency

No separate prop reference was built for the sapling, unlike the FORGE spiral. A bare-root
seedling is generic enough that small variation between shots reads as natural rather than as
an error. The consistency insurance went into the **terrain** instead, which is where the
risk actually sits on this film — the bare slope and the forested slope must read as one place.
