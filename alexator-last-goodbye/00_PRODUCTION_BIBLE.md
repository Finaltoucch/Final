# THE LAST GOODBYE — Production Bible
ALEXATOR **012 — Last Goodbye** · ancient Egypt · queen & palace servant

## Concept
A queen and a palace servant love each other across a distance measured in
stone. The film tracks that distance closing, then watches it open forever in
a single moment where she is close enough to touch him and does not turn her
head. No dialogue. Told entirely in eyelines and proximity.

A small BLUE FAIENCE LOTUS BEAD passes between them once. It appears three
times; the third time is the ending. Shot 10 looks like she abandoned him.
Shot 11 reveals she kept the bead. That reversal is the film.

## BRIEF COMPLIANCE NOTES
- The queen is an INVENTED FICTIONAL CHARACTER. Every prompt states she is
  NOT Cleopatra, NOT Nefertiti and NOT any real or historical person, because
  the client brief bars real-person reference.
- Carved hieroglyphic relief appears as architecture only, never as a hero
  element, never legible. Overlay text, captions, logos and watermarks are
  banned outright in every prompt.

## LOCKED SPECS — paste VERBATIM into every prompt showing that subject

**THE QUEEN** — 23, Egyptian, North African features, warm deep bronze-brown
skin with visible pores. Strong straight dark eyebrows, high cheekbones, a
straight nose, a full mouth, a long neck held very still. Dark brown almond
eyes rimmed with BLACK KOHL extended into a fine point at the outer corner
and a line along the lower lid. A heavy BLACK EGYPTIAN WIG, glossy and dense,
BLUNT STRAIGHT FRINGE across the brow, falling straight to just below her
shoulders with the ends cut level, two thin gold bands. A narrow
FLOOR-LENGTH WHITE PLEATED LINEN SHEATH DRESS with two narrow shoulder
straps. A BROAD EGYPTIAN COLLAR of concentric bead rows in GOLD, DEEP LAPIS
BLUE, TURQUOISE and CARNELIAN RED with a gold edge. A plain GOLD CIRCLET at
the brow with a small rearing cobra. Broad gold armlets high on both upper
arms, gold bracelets on both wrists. Flat gold sandals. AN INVENTED
FICTIONAL CHARACTER — NOT Cleopatra, NOT Nefertiti, NOT any real person.

**THE SERVANT** — 25, Egyptian, North African features, warm medium-brown
skin, sun-darkened across the shoulders and forearms. Lean and hard from
manual labour, wiry working muscle. Dark brown eyes, straight brows, a
straight nose, clean shaven. Short cropped BLACK HAIR close to the skull,
NOT a wig. An UNDYED OFF-WHITE LINEN SHENDYT KILT with a DIAGONAL
OVERLAPPING FRONT FOLD and a KNOTTED LINEN WAIST TIE, to just above the knee.
BARE-CHESTED and BAREFOOT. A single thin plain leather cord at his neck.
NO gold, NO jewellery, NO collar, NO armlets, NO sandals, NO decoration.

**THE PALACE** — a RICH PAINTED ROYAL RESIDENCE, never a bare grey temple.
Slender columns carved as bundled LOTUS AND PAPYRUS STEMS painted deep
EGYPTIAN BLUE, GREEN and GOLD with RED and YELLOW OCHRE detail, capitals
opening into painted lotus petals. Cream lime-plastered walls with VIVID
PAINTED DADO BANDS of blue lotus, green papyrus and red and yellow borders,
and panels of TURQUOISE FAIENCE TILE. A polished painted floor showing a
stylised pool with blue water, fish and lotus pads, worn in the traffic
lanes. White linen hangings between the columns. Tall alabaster oil lamps.
Low carved furniture with gold fittings and woven cushions in red, blue and
gold. Paint hand-applied and slightly worn, plaster lightly chipped, real
dust in the air.

**THE BEAD** — a small LOTUS-FLOWER-SHAPED FAIENCE BEAD about 2 cm across,
saturated EGYPTIAN BLUE shading to TURQUOISE at the raised petal edges,
glossy with fine age crazing and tiny chips at the petal tips, a narrow
stringing hole at the base. Completely plain — no letters, no hieroglyphs,
no symbols. The only cool colour in a warm film.

## RULES CARRIED FORWARD
1. Full specs verbatim in every prompt. Never shorten on a rebuild.
2. State blocking explicitly — which way each person faces, and against the
   FRAME not just the world. Shot 05 took three attempts because "passing
   each other" was not stated as "she walks toward camera, he walks away".
3. When a beat keeps failing as a wide, make it an INSERT. Shot 05 became
   two hands and a gap of lit air, and worked immediately.
4. Ask for A PHOTOGRAPH explicitly — "shot on 35mm film, real skin texture,
   NOT a 3D render, NOT CGI, NOT ArchViz". Saturated-colour prompts drift
   toward architectural-render aesthetics without it.
5. Avoid weapon-adjacent wording. Shot 08 failed twice on "trumpets raised
   and ready" and "standards in formation"; it rendered first time once
   rewritten as a waiting empty litter.
6. NEVER assemble or deliver until every shot has been LOOKED AT as a still.
   `sandbox_exec` returns images natively via `image_paths`.
7. `generate_image_batch` MUST carry `aspect_ratio:"9:16"`.
