# ADRENALINE — THE SMOKEJUMPER
ALEXATOR "021 Adrenaline". Vertical 9:16 AI music video.

## CONCEPT
A smokejumper in the door of a plane above a burning ridge. On the drop he is
gone — the frame falls away with him, the canopy cracks open, and he comes down
through smoke onto the black. He lands, cuts free, shoulders a tool and walks
INTO it, and cuts a line of bare earth that stops the fire.

MEANING: running toward the thing everyone else runs from. Not spectacle —
the job. The last shot is not the fire, it is the line that held.

A 9:16 frame is a falling frame, so the format does the work. Nothing we have
made has had this much velocity.
